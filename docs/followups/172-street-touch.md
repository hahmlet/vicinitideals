# 172. A lot line read as a street when only a few feet touch a street end

From fg-rate-A. The screen classed a 30.7 ft west line as a side street; only 6.3 ft touches the end of SE Ash St.
Fix: the drive/court may only reach a street through the stretch of line that actually touches the street (not the whole
line); a street touch narrower than the drive width cannot carry a drive.
