Gresham 7.0420(I)(2) (flats/provenance/docs/or/multnomah/gresham/7/7.0420.txt, Transparency): "Clear glass
in windows and/or doors shall occupy a minimum of 5 percent of all other facades" (VT >= 60%). Applies in
LDR/TR/MDR/CMF/CMU/SC/SC-RJ/DCC/DMU/DTM/DRL etc. (7.0420 opening list).
Oregon ORSC R302.1 (item 183 findings, ../vicinitideals-worktrees/side-wall-openings.findings.md): a wall
under 3 ft from a side/rear lot line may have NO openings.
Conflict: rel7 has 40 Gresham GREENs whose pod END wall sits < 3 ft from a side/rear line (SC 20, CMU 17,
CMF 2, SC-RJ 1; e.g. 1N3E31DD  -01700 at 1.18 ft). If "5 percent of all other facades" means EACH facade,
that end wall can never carry its glass -> those greens are false unless the pod can slide to >= 3 ft.
If it means the total of the other facades (I(1) says "total" for the street side; I(2) does not), the back
long wall can carry it and nothing moves.
Build (after the reading is settled): in Gresham 7.0420 zones, hold any exposed end wall >= 3 ft from
side/rear lines in placement; lots that cannot -> YELLOW + flag (or RED if read "each" strictly and no
placement works). Party walls between homes are not facades. Side Wall Articulation 7.0420(H) is met by
option (a) on a 25 ft end wall (< 35 ft), so its 10% glass option is never needed.
Per end wall at 5%: 25 ft x 20 ft = 25 sq ft of glass (~3 x 36x48, glass ~9 sq ft each); at 26 ft tall 32.5
sq ft (~4).
