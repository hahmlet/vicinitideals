# 170. Hillsboro SCR 5 ft maximum side setback: one wall or both?

From fg-rate-B: Hillsboro SCR greens rest on reading the 5 ft MAXIMUM side setback as applying to one wall. Not in the
rulings register (it is a reading, not a Steph ruling). A both-walls reading would turn nearly every SCR-MD green red. Read it.
