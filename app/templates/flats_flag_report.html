Room to spare on green lots

For each limit a green lot passes, how much room it had left. The limits the most lots come close to are at the top: "within 10%" counts the lots whose room is less than a tenth of the limit itself (under 3 ft of a 30 ft height limit, say). The fit is the room left inside the front and rear setbacks; the room side to side is not measured yet.

Pod width

What happens to the colours if the building is made wider or narrower: whether it still fits on the lot, and, where the run kept how much room each lot had, whether the bigger building still clears the coverage, floor area, paving and open space limits. Other limits (lot size, density, parking) do not change with the building's size. A lot short by under a foot on the fit stays yellow, as the colour rule says.
