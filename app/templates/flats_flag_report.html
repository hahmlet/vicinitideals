Room to spare on green lots

For each limit a green lot passes, how much room it had left. The limits the most lots come close to are at the top: "within 10%" counts the lots whose room is less than a tenth of the limit itself (under 3 ft of a 30 ft height limit, say). The fit is the room left inside the front and rear setbacks. Below each design's table, how much bigger the building could be on the lot's own shape: wider, deeper, and both at once -- on an oddly shaped lot (a wedge, an L) there can be room to grow each way alone but less to grow both ways together.

Pod width

What happens to the colours if the building is made wider or narrower (along the street): whether it still fits on the lot, read off the room each lot had that way on its own shape, and, where the run kept how much room each lot had on each limit, whether the bigger building still clears the coverage, floor area, paving and open space limits. Other limits (lot size, density, parking) do not change with the building's size. A lot short by under a foot on the fit stays yellow, as the colour rule says. On a run screened before the room each way was kept, only the front-to-back fit is read.
