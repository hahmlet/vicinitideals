Plans

A plan is a building we can actually deliver — its size, its unit count, its height, how it parks, and how its units are platted. This page asks what a lot would have to be for each plan to be legal in every zone we have encoded. No parcel is involved: it is the plan against the code.

Platted as One lot (quadplex) Four lots (townhouses) Under ORS 92.031 a middle housing land division is judged against the standards for the original lot, so the numbers are usually the same — a city may not charge four minimum lot sizes for splitting one building. Where a city states its own townhouse lot standards, they show here instead.
