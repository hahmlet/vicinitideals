Sec. 17.72.00. - Purpose.


The purpose of congregate living is to provide housing alternatives for senior citizens, handicapped persons, and others living in a congregate living facility.
The standards set forth in this section are intended to ensure that congregate living developments provide a minimum of services and facilities to accommodate the needs of the occupants and to relieve any possible detrimental effects of the development on surrounding properties.

(Ord. No. 2024-01, § 1(Exh.
A), 2-20-2024)
