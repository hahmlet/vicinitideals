Sec. 17.100.180. - Intersections.


A.

Intersections. Streets shall intersect at angles of 75 to 105 degrees.
In no circumstances shall a proposed intersection of two new streets be approved at an angle of less than 75 degrees.
No more than two streets shall intersect at any one point.
Intersections shall be spaced to maintain a minimum of 150 feet between the nearest edges of the two rights-of-way.
Driveways located on a collector or arterial street shall maintain a minimum distance of 150 feet between the nearest edges of the driveway and a right-of-way that intersects with the collector or arterial street.

B.

Curve Radius. All streets shall have a minimum curve radius (at intersections of rights-of-way) of 28 feet, per Oregon Fire Code standards for fire apparatus access roads.
