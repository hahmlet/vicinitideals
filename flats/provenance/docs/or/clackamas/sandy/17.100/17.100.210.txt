Sec. 17.100.210. - Street lighting.


Installation of a complete lighting system (including conduits, wiring, bases, poles, arms, and fixtures) shall be the financial responsibility of the property owner completing the partition, replat, or subdivision on all cul-de-sacs, local streets, collector streets, and arterial streets.
Standards and specifications for street lighting shall conform to IESNA roadway illumination standards and the 2020 City of Sandy Streetlighting Guidelines.
