Sec. 17.100.200. - Street surfacing.


Public streets, including alleys, within a partition, replat, or subdivision shall be improved in accordance with the requirements of the Oregon Standard Specifications AASHTO Green Book or OSSC standards.
All streets shall be paved with asphaltic concrete or Portland cement concrete surfacing.
