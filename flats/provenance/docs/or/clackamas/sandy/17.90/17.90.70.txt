Sec. 17.90.70. - Expiration of approval.


Design Review approval shall be void after two years from the date of the Final Order, unless the applicant has submitted plans for building permit approval or demolition approval, as applicable, within this timeframe.
The Director may grant one extension through a Type I procedure, not to exceed one year, upon a written request from the applicant prior to the expiration date of the approval.

(Ord. No. 2024-01, § 1(Exh. A), 2-20-2024)
