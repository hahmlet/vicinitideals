Sec. 17.54.60. - BVO applicability.


Development and land use within the Bornstedt Village Overlay district, as shown on the City of Sandy Zoning Map (reflecting Figure 5 in the Bornstedt Village Specific Area Plan), shall be in conformance with the provisions outlined in this chapter.
Cascadia Village Subdivisions #1 through #6 are exempt from Sections 17.54.70—17.54.110.
