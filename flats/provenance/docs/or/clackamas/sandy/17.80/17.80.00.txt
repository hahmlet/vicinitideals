Sec. 17.80.00. - Intent.


The requirement of additional special setbacks for development on arterial or collector is intended to provide better light, air, and vision on more heavily traveled streets.
The additional setback on substandard streets will protect collector and arterial streets and permit the eventual widening of streets.

(Ord. No. 2024-01, § 1(Exh. A), 2-20-2024)
