Sec. 17.80.20. - Specific setbacks.


Any structure located on streets listed above or identified in the 2023 City of Sandy Transportation System Plan as arterials or collectors shall have a minimum setback of 20 feet measured from the property line.
This applies to applicable front, rear, and side yards.

(Ord. No. 2024-01, § 1(Exh.
A), 2-20-2024)
