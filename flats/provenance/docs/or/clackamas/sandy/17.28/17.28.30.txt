Sec. 17.28.30. - Scope of review.


Except where a de novo hearing is required, an appeal is limited to a review of the record and a hearing for receipt of oral arguments regarding the record.
At its discretion, the hearing body may allow an appeal to include new evidence on specific issues, or it may allow a full de novo hearing.

(Ord. No. 2022-07, § 3(Exh.
C), 5-2-2022; Ord. No. 2024-01, § 1(Exh.
A), 2-20-2024)
