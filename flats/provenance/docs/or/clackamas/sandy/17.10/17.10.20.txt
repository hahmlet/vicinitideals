Sec. 17.10.20. - Meaning of common words.


A.

All words used in the present tense include the future tense.

B.

All words used in the plural include the singular, and all words used in the singular include the plural unless the context clearly indicates to the contrary.

C.

The word "shall" is mandatory and the word "may" is permissive.

D.

The word "building" includes the word "structure."

E.

The phrase "used for" includes the phrases "arranged for," "designed for," "intended for," "maintained for," and "occupied for."

F.

The word "land" and "property" are used interchangeably unless the context clearly indicates to the contrary.

G.

The word "person" may be taken for persons, associations, firms, partnerships or corporations.
