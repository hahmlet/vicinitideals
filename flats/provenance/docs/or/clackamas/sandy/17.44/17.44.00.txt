Sec. 17.44.00. - Intent.


This district is intended to provide for a wide range of commercial uses and related services and businesses, which require large land areas for structures and parking facilities, and rely on direct automobile access.
This district is not intended exclusively for residential uses, although mixed-use developments are encouraged.

(Ord. No. 2024-01, § 1(Exh.
A), 2-20-2024)
