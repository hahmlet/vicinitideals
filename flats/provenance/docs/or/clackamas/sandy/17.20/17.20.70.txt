Sec. 17.20.70. - Notice of decision.


Following the signing of the Final Order and Findings of Fact, the Director shall issue a Notice of Decision that describes the decision of the hearing authority, a reference to findings leading to it, any conditions of approval, and application appeal period deadline.
Subject to any requirements of state law, the notice of decision will be issued to persons who submitted written testimony and were not in attendance at the hearing, in addition to those persons who are entitled to receive a notice of decision by other provisions of this Code.

(Ord. No. 2024-01, § 1(Exh.
A), 2-20-2024)
