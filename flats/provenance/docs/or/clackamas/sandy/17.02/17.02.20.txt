Sec. 17.02.20. - The Planning Commission.


The Planning Commission shall be appointed in accordance with the Sandy Municipal Code.
The Commission shall have the powers and duties provided therein and provided by this Code.
The Commission shall also hear and act on appeals resulting from alleged errors in orders, requirements, decisions, and interpretations of the Director or designated administrative officers charged with the enforcement of this Code and such other matters as required by this Code.

(Ord. No. 2024-01, § 1(Exh. A), 2-20-2024)
