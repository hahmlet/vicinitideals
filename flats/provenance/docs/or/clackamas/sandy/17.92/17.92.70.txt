Sec. 17.92.70. - Landscaping in the public right-of-way.


The planter strip in the public right-of-way shall include street trees spaced in accordance with Section 17.92.30, in accordance with the adopted street tree list, and at sizes in accordance with Section 17.92.50.
In addition to street trees, the planter strip shall also include other landscaping, such as groundcover, turf, or wood chips to fully cover all exposed soils.
Landscaping within the street right-of-way shall not count as part of the site area percentage to be landscaped in accordance with Section 17.92.20.

(Ord. No. 2024-01, § 1(Exh. A), 2-20-2024)
