Sec. 17.84.10. - Exceptions—Improvements.


Single family residential, accessory dwelling unit, and duplex development on existing lots with existing public improvements are exempt from this chapter, with the exception of 17.84.30 Pedestrian and Bicyclist Requirements.

(Ord. No. 2024-01, § 1(Exh. A), 2-20-2024; Ord. No. 2025-32, § 1(Exh. A), 11-17-2025)
