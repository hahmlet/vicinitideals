Sec. 17.36.00. - Intent.


This district is intended to implement the Low Density Residential Comprehensive Plan designation by providing low-density residential development.
It is to be used as a transition between the Single-Family Residential zone and the higher density zones.
The uses are to be fully serviced by public facilities.
Density shall not be less than five or more than eight units per net acre.

(Ord. No. 2022-07, § 6(Exh.
F), 5-2-2022; Ord. No. 2024-01, § 1(Exh.
A), 2-20-2024)
