Sec. 17.66.190. - Effective period of approval.


Approval of an adjustment or variance shall be effective for a two-year period from the date of approval, unless substantial construction has taken place.
The Director (Type I and Type II) or Planning Commission (Type III) may grant a one-year extension if the applicant requests such an extension prior to expiration of the initial time limit.

(Ord. No. 2024-01, § 1(Exh.
A), 2-20-2024)
