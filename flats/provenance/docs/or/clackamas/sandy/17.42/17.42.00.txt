Sec. 17.42.00. - Intent.


This district is intended to provide the community with a mix of retail, personal services, offices, and residential uses in the city's historic commercial core.
This district is not intended for intensive automobile or industrial uses.

This commercial district is intended for civic uses and to provide all basic services and amenities required to keep the downtown a vital center of the community.
While the district does not permit new low density building types, it is not intended to preclude dwelling units in buildings containing commercial uses.

(Ord. No. 2024-01, § 1(Exh. A), 2-20-2024)
