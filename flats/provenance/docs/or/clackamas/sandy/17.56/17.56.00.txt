Sec. 17.56.00. - Intent.


The intent of this chapter is to comply with Statewide Planning Goal 7 (Natural Hazards) by minimizing seismic and landslide hazards, and soil erosion associated with development on steep or unstable slopes.
Development may be permitted on steep slopes, provided that the recommendations of approved studies are implemented as conditions of building permit or land use approval.

(Ord. No. 2024-01, § 1(Exh.
A), 2-20-2024)
