Sec. 17.22.60. - Publication of notices.


A general legal notice containing a link to the City's webpage that lists upcoming public hearings shall be published in a newspaper of general circulation at least once per month.
Upcoming Type III and IV public hearings, except for hardship permits per Chapter 17.70, shall be posted to the City's social media page a minimum of five days prior to the hearing.

(Ord. No. 2022-07, § 3(Exh.
C), 5-2-2022; Ord. No. 2024-01, § 1(Exh.
A), 2-20-2024)
