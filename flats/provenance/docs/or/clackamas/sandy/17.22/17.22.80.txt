Sec. 17.22.80. - List of property owners.


The applicant shall provide a certified list of property owners and mailing labels as required by notice provisions of this Code.
Unless otherwise provided, addresses for a mailed notice shall be obtained from the County's real property tax records.
Unless the address is on file with the Director, a person whose name is not in the tax records at the time of filing of an application, or of initiating other action not based on an application, need not be furnished mailed notice.

(Ord. No. 2024-01, § 1(Exh. A), 2-20-2024)
