Sec. 17.04.70. - Text amendments.


This Code may be amended whenever the public necessity, convenience, and general welfare require such amendment and where it conforms to the Sandy Comprehensive Plan and any other applicable policies.
A.

Initiation. Initiation of an amendment may be accomplished by one of the following methods:

1.

Majority vote of the City Council; or

2.

Majority vote of the Planning Commission; or

3.

Citizens can request that the City Council initiate an amendment.
Council may either initiate and direct staff to proceed or may deny and direct the applicant proceed with an amendment.

B.

Review of Text Amendments. The Planning Commission and City Council shall review proposed amendments in accordance with the legislative provisions of Chapter 17.20—Public Hearings.
