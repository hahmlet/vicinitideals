Sec. 17.04.50. - Revival.


The repeal of Sandy Ordinance 11-80 as amended, does not thereby revive any provision, ordinance, or section that was in effect prior to the adoption or amendment of Sandy Ordinance 11-80 as amended.
This repeal does not affect any punishment, penalty, or fine incurred before the repeal took place or any prosecution or proceeding commenced or pending prior to the adoption of this Code.
