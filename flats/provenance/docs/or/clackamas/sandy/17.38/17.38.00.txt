Sec. 17.38.00. - Intent.


This district is intended to implement the Medium Density Residential Comprehensive Plan designation by providing for medium density single-family and multi-family uses in suitable locations, where public sewer, water, and other services are readily accessible.
All development shall also provide access to the surrounding neighborhood with excellent linkage between residential areas, schools, and parks.
Density shall not be less than eight or more than 14 units per net acre.

(Ord. No. 2022-07, § 7(Exh.
G), 5-2-2022; Ord. No. 2024-01, § 1(Exh.
A), 2-20-2024)
