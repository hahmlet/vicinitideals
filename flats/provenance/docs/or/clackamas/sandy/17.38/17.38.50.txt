Sec. 17.38.50. - Additional requirements.


A.

Design review as specified in Chapter 17.90 is required for all uses.

B.

Lots with 40 feet or less of street frontage shall be accessed by a rear alley or a shared private driveway.

C.

Zero lot line dwellings.
Prior to building permit approval, the applicant shall submit a recorded easement between the subject property and the abutting lot next to the yard having the zero setback.
This easement shall be sufficient to guarantee rights for maintenance purposes of structures and yard, but in no case shall it be less than five feet in width.

(Ord. No. 2024-01, § 1(Exh.
A), 2-20-2024)
