Sec. 17.50.00. - Intent.


It is the intent of this district to provide locations in suitable areas for manufacturing and warehousing business, or other commercial uses that do not depend on high visibility.
Commercial or retail uses must be compatible with an environment that includes heavy truck traffic and outdoor storage of industrial materials.
Because building design standards are less restrictive in this zone than in other zones, buildings (regardless of use) shall be screened from view from arterial streets and highways.
