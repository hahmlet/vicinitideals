Sec. 17.96.00. - Intent.


These provisions are established to ensure a safe and healthful living environment for residents of manufactured dwelling parks and to ensure that a manufactured dwelling park can provide affordable quality housing compatible with adjacent land uses.
In addition, these provisions are intended to ensure compliance with State regulations governing review of manufactured dwelling park development.

(Ord. No. 2024-01, § 1(Exh. A), 2-20-2024)
