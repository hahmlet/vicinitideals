Sec. 17.82.10. - Applicability.


This chapter applies to all residential development located adjacent to a transit street.
A transit street is defined as any street designated as a collector or arterial, or other streets meeting the "transit street" definition in the 2020 Sandy Transit Master Plan.

(Ord. No. 2024-01, § 1(Exh.
A), 2-20-2024)
