Sec. 17.102.00. - Intent.


A.

This chapter is intended to conserve and replenish the ecological, aesthetic and economic benefits of urban forests, by regulating tree removal on properties greater than one acre within the Sandy Urban Growth Boundary.

B.

This chapter is intended to facilitate planned urban development as prescribed by the Sandy Comprehensive Plan, through the appropriate location of harvest areas, landing and yarding areas, roads and drainage facilities.

C.

This chapter shall be construed in a manner consistent with Chapter 17.60 Flood and Slope Hazard Overlay District.
In cases of conflict, Chapter 17.60 shall prevail.

(Ord. No. 2024-01, § 1(Exh.
A), 2-20-2024)
