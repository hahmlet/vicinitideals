Sec. 17.102.80. - Enforcement.


The provisions of Chapter 17.06, Enforcement, shall apply to tree removal that is not in conformance with this chapter.
Each unauthorized tree removal shall be considered a separate offense for purposes of assigning penalties under Section 17.06.80.
At least 50 percent of funds generated as a result of enforcement of this chapter shall be dedicated to the Urban Forestry Fund established under Section 17.102.100, below, with the remaining 50 percent dedicated to the administrative costs associated with overseeing the fine.

(Ord. No. 2024-01, § 1(Exh.
A), 2-20-2024)
