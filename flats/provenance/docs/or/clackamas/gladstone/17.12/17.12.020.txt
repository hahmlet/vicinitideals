17.12.020 Uses allowed outright.

In an R-5 zoning district, the following uses and their accessory uses are allowed outright:

(1) Single-household dwelling, including a manufactured dwelling.

(2) Middle housing.

(3) Manufactured dwelling park, subject to GMC Section 17.62.070 (Manufactured dwelling park).

(4) Family child care home.

(5) Single-room occupancy, up to six units on each lot or parcel.

(6) Multi-household dwelling, five to eight units on one parcel within Gladstone Town Center.

(7) Mixed-use development within Gladstone Town Center.
Commercial uses permitted in the mixed-use development include those listed as permitted in the C-2 zoning district, GMC Section 17.18.020.
