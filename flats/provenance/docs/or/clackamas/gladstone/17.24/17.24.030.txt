17.24.030 Residential accessory uses.

Accessory uses allowed in a residential zoning district shall be allowed in connection with single-family, two-family and multifamily dwellings in this zoning district.
Such accessory uses shall comply with the standards applicable to accessory uses allowed in the MR zoning district.

Statutory Reference: ORS Chs. 197, 227.

History: Ord. 1131 §2, 1990; Ord. 1323 §1, 2002; Ord. 1535 §1 (Exh.
A), 2024.
