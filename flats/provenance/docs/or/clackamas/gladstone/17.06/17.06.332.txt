17.06.332 Middle housing land division.

“Middle housing land division” means the partition or subdivision of a lot of record that is developed, or proposed to be developed, with middle housing dwelling units.
The type of middle housing developed on the original lot of record is not altered by a middle housing land division.
Middle housing land divisions are expedited land divisions that are subject to the process outlined in ORS 197.375.

Statutory Reference: ORS Ch. 197, Ch. 375.
