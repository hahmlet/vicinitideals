17.06.535 Temporary structure real estate offices.

“Temporary structure real estate offices” means a temporary structure located in a subdivision used for the promotion of a sale of residential units in a developing subdivision.

Statutory Reference: ORS Ch. 197, Ch. 27

History: Ord. 1131 §2, 1990; Ord. 1535 §1 (Exh. A), 2024.
