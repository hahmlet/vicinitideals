17.06.105 Comprehensive Plan.

“Comprehensive Plan” means the official plan adopted by the city for the guidance of growth and improvement, including modifications or refinements which may be made from time to time.

Statutory Reference: ORS Ch. 197, Ch. 227

History: Ord. 1131 §2, 1990; Ord. 1535 §1 (Exh. A), 2024.
