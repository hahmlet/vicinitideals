17.06.418 Porch, front.

“Front porch” means a front entrance to a dwelling covered by a separate roof or an extension of the dwelling roof.
Enclosure walls shall extend no more than half the distance from the front porch to the porch eaves.
A front porch shall be used only for recreational, outdoor living purposes and not as a carport, garage, storage room or habitable room.

Statutory Reference: ORS Ch. 197, Ch. 227

History: Ord. 1289 §1, 2000; Ord. 1535 §1 (Exh. A), 2024.
