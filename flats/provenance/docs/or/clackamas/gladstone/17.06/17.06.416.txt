17.06.416 Plat.

“Plat” means a final map and other writing containing all the descriptions, locations, specifications, dedications, provisions and information concerning a subdivision or partition.

Statutory Reference: ORS Ch. 197, Ch. 227

History: Ord. 1323 §1, 2002; Ord. 1535 §1 (Exh.
A), 2024.
