17.06.013 Accessory dwelling unit.

“Accessory dwelling unit” means an additional, smaller, subordinate dwelling unit located on the same lot as a detached or attached single-household dwelling or multi-household dwelling.
An accessory dwelling may be created as a detached structure or within, or as an addition to, a detached single-household dwelling or accessory structure.
