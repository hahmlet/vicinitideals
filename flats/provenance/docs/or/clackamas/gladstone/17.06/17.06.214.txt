17.06.214 Home for the aged.

“Home for the aged” means a supervised personal care facility where staff provide services to residents.
A home for the aged is not a group home, foster home, hospital, or nursing home.

Statutory Reference: ORS Ch. 197, Ch. 227

History: Ord. 1515 §2 (Exh.
B), 2022; Ord. 1535 §1 (Exh.
