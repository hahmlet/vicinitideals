17.06.200 Foster home.

“Foster home” means any dwelling occupied and maintained by a person or persons licensed or certified by the state or other authorized agency to provide extended or temporary care, food, and lodging in such home for not more than seven dependent individuals beyond the number defined as a household.
A foster home is not a hospital, nursing home, or home for the aged as defined in Oregon Revised Statutes, nor does it include a halfway house, work release center, or any other domiciliary facility for persons released from any penal or correctional institution.
