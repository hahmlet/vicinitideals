17.06.141 Dwelling, multi-household.

“Multi-household dwelling” means a building, designed for occupancy by five or more households living independently of each other.

Statutory Reference: ORS Ch. 197, Ch. 227

History: Ord. 1131 §2, 1990; Ord. 1515 §2 (Exh.
B), 2022; Ord. 1535 §1 (Exh.
A), 2024. Formerly 17.06.140.
