17.06.142 Dwelling, detached single-household.

“Detached single-household dwelling” means a detached building containing one dwelling unit located on a single lot.

Statutory Reference: ORS Ch. 197, Ch. 227

History: Ord. 1131 §2, 1990; Ord. 1515 §2 (Exh.
B), 2022; Ord. 1535 §1 (Exh.
A), 2024. Formerly 17.06.145.
