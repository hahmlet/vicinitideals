17.06.146 Townhouse.

“Townhouse” means a dwelling unit that is part of a row of two or more attached dwelling units, where each unit is located on an individual lot and shares at least one common wall with an adjacent dwelling unit.

History: Ord. 1515 §2 (Exh.
B), 2022; Ord. 1535 §1 (Exh.
A), 2024.
