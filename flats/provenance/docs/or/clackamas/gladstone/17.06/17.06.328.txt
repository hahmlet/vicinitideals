17.06.328 Manufactured dwelling.

“Manufactured dwelling” means a residential trailer, mobile home or manufactured home.

Statutory Reference: ORS Chs. 197, 227

History: Ord. 1171 §1(A), 1993; Ord. 1179 §11(A), 1993; Ord. 1515 §2 (Exh. B), 2022; Ord. 1518 §1 (Exh. A), 2022; Ord. 1535 §1 (Exh. A), 2024.
