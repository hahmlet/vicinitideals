17.06.231 Household.

“Household” means one person or two or more persons occupying a dwelling unit as their usual place of residence.
This definition shall not apply to group homes, foster homes, or homes for the aged.

Statutory Reference: ORS Ch. 197, Ch. 227

History: Ord. 1131 §2, 1990; Ord. 1515 §6 (Exh. B), 2022; Ord. 1518 §1 (Exh. A), 2022; Ord. 1535 §1 (Exh. A), 2024.
