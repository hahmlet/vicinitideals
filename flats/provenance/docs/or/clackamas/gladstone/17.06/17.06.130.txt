17.06.130 Drinking establishment.

“Drinking establishment” means a place of business which requires a permit and/or license from the Oregon Liquor and Cannabis Commission for the dispensing of liquor for consumption upon the premises.

Statutory Reference: ORS Ch. 197, Ch. 227

History: Ord. 1131 §2, 1990; Ord. 1535 §1 (Exh. A), 2024.
