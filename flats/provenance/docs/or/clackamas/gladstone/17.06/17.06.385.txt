17.06.385 Parcel.

“Parcel” means a unit of land that is created by a partitioning of land.
For the purposes of this title, “parcel” includes “lot” and “lot of record” unless the context otherwise requires.

Statutory Reference: ORS Ch. 197, Ch. 227

History: Ord. 1131 §2, 1990; Ord. 1323 §1, 2002; Ord. 1535 §1 (Exh.
A), 2024.
