17.06.148 Cottage cluster.

“Cottage cluster” means a grouping of no fewer than four detached dwelling units per acre with a footprint of less than 900 square feet each that includes a common courtyard on a single lot or on individual lots.

History: Ord. 1515 §2 (Exh. B), 2022; Ord. 1535 §1 (Exh. A), 2024.
