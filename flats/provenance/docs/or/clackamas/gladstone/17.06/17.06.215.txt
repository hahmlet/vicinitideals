17.06.215 Group home.

“Group home” means any home occupied and maintained by a person or persons licensed by the state or other appropriate agency to provide extended or temporary care, food and lodging in such home for from eight to 10 dependent individuals beyond the number defined as a household.
A group home is not a foster home, hospital, nursing home or home for the aged as defined in Oregon Revised Statutes, nor does it include a halfway house, work release center, or any domiciliary facility for persons released from any penal or correctional institution.

Statutory Reference: ORS Ch. 197, Ch. 227
