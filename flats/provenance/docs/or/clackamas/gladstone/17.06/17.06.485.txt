17.06.485 School, primary, elementary, junior high or high.

“Primary, elementary, junior high or high school” includes public, private or parochial but not nursery school, kindergarten or day nursery, except when operated in conjunction with a school.

Statutory Reference: ORS Ch. 197, Ch. 227

History: Ord. 1131 §2, 1990; Ord. 1535 §1 (Exh.
A), 2024.
