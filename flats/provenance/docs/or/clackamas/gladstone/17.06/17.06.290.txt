17.06.290 Lot line, front.

“Front lot line” means a lot line separating a lot from a street other than an alley.
In the case of a corner lot, either of the lot lines separating the lot from a street other than an alley may be designated by the property owner as the front lot line with the other such lot line designated as a street side lot line.
Except as modified by GMC Section 17.76.040(5), the front lot line of a flag lot, for the purposes of determining minimum yard requirements, shall be a line within the boundaries of the lot by a distance equal to the width of the narrow deeded strip of land or easement providing access to the lot from the street.
The line shall be parallel to the lot line that extends from the street to the lot line opposite and most distant from the street.

Statutory Reference: ORS Ch. 197, Ch. 227
