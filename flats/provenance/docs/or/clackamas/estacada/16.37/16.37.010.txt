16.37.010 Purpose

The CMU zone implements the Central Mixed-Use Comprehensive Plan policies.
It is intended to establish small-footprint commercial uses, intermixed with denser residential uses, that cater primarily to customers in nearby residential areas and that do not detract from Downtown, while developing Eagle Creek Rd as a tree-lined, pedestrian and bicycle-friendly corridor.
