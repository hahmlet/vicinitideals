16.34.010 Purpose

The R-C zone implements the Riverfront Commercial Comprehensive Plan policies.
It is intended to promote local tourism and history, outdoor recreation, and the use of adjacent park facilities, the Clackamas River, and connecting multi-use paths with a variety of commercial uses that attract local and out-of-town customers while enhancing the area's natural aesthetics and protecting adjacent open spaces.
