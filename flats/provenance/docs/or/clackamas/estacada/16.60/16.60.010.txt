16.60.010 Clear Vision Areas

1 A clear vision area, as defined in EMC 16.08.010, shall be maintained on the corners of all property at the intersection of two streets or from the intersection of a private road easement and a public street.

2 A clear vision area shall contain no planting, fence, wall, sign, banner, structure or temporary or permanent obstruction exceeding 2.5 feet or thirty (30) inches in height measured from the top of the curb, or, where no curb exists, from the established street center line grade.
Trees exceeding this height may be located in this area, provided that all branches and foliage are removed to a height of eight feet above grade.

(Prior code § 10.234)
