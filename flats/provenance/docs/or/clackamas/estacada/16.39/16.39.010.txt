16.39.010 Purpose

The M-1 zone is intended for the expansion of light industrial uses.
It permits wholly enclosed light industrial uses and commercial uses which are compatible to the surrounding area, subject to conditions.

(Ord. 2000-26 § 6 (part): prior code § 10.225 (part))
