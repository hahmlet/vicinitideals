16.12.050 Zoning Of Annexed Areas

Areas annexed to the city shall be zoned in accordance with the adopted comprehensive plan and may be reviewed by the planning commission to determine if the proposed use is compatible with the proposed zoning designation.
The planning commission may recommend a different zone designation to the city council for consideration during the annexation procedure.

(Prior code § 10.215)
