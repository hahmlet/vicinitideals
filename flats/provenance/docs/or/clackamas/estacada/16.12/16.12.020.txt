16.12.020 Location Of Zones

The boundaries for the zones listed above are appended to the ordinance codified in this chapter as an appendix to this development code and marked as such with the area or areas of every zone, particularly described and are also indicated on the map entitled "Estacada, Oregon Zoning."
Official maps are on file in the office of the city recorder in a book or place kept for that purpose and open to public inspection.
These maps are incorporated into and made part of this development code.

(Prior code § 10.212)
