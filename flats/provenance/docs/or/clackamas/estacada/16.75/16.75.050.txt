16.75.050 Walkway Width And Surface

Walkways shall be constructed of concrete, asphalt, brick or masonry pavers, or other durable surface, as approved by the City Manager or designee, and not less than five feet wide.
Multi-use paths (i.e., designed for shared use by bicyclists and pedestrians) shall be concrete or asphalt and shall conform to city transportation standards.
