16.75.020 Standards

Developments shall conform to all of the following standards for pedestrian access and circulation:

1 Continuous Walkway System.
A pedestrian walkway system shall extend throughout the development site and connect to adjacent sidewalks, if any, and to all future phases of the development, as applicable.

2 Safe, Direct, and Convenient.

The walkway network connects to all primary building entrances consistent with Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) requirements.
