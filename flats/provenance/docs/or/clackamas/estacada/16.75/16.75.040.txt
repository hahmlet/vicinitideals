16.75.040 Crosswalks

Where a walkway crosses a parking area or driveway ("crosswalk"), it shall be clearly marked with contrasting paving materials (e.g., pavers, light-color concrete inlay between asphalt, or similar contrasting material) or painted crosswalk striping.
The crosswalk may be part of a speed table to improve driver-visibility of pedestrians.
