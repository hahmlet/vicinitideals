16.32.010 Purpose

The C-2 zone is intended for a mixture of office, retail, personal or business service, plus allowing residential uses.
C-2 was created to promote the most productive capacity of property.
Several areas of the city have developed into a combination of residential and commercial use zones.
The purpose of this zoning district is to recognize and to continue this development pattern.

(Ord. 2000-26 § 5 (part): prior code § 10.224 (part); Ord. No. 2016-003, § 1(Exh.
A), 5-23-2016)
