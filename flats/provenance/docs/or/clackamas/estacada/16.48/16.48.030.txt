16.48.030 Historic Resource Review Committee

1 The historic resources review committee shall consist of five members.
Until such time as the city council appoints specific members to this committee, the planning commission shall act in this capacity.

2 When a specific committee is appointed, members shall serve for three years, except the first term, of which two members shall serve for three years; three members shall serve for two years.

3 All demolition and alteration permit requests shall be reviewed by the committee.
These permit requests must be reviewed and approved by the historic resource review committee prior to a permit for demolition or alteration being approved.

(Prior code 10.228(B))
