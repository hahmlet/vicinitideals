16.35.010 Purpose

The H-C zone implements the Highway Commercial Comprehensive Plan policies.
It is intended to provide for large-footprint and vehicle-oriented commercial uses that are not appropriate for Downtown or other commercial areas, while ensuring that new development is appropriately screened and restricting residential uses, industrial uses, and excessive signage.
