16.38.010 Purpose

The O-C zone implements the Outdoor Commercial Comprehensive Plan policies.
It is intended to preserve and enhance the area's natural aesthetic and establish commercial and public uses that attract local and out-of-town visitors, while protecting natural wetlands and adjacent residential land uses and ensuring that the area's commercial uses do not detract from Downtown.
