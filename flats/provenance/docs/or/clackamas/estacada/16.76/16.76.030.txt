16.76.030 Substitution For Additional Bicycle Parking

For every five bicycle parking spaces provided for uses in the City's Downtown (D), General Commercial (C-1), Commercial Residential (C-2), and Light Industrial (M-1) zones that are beyond the required minimum and that meet the City's long-term bicycle parking standards, the vehicle parking requirement for the use is reduced by one space.
This additional bicycle parking may substitute for no more than 25 percent of required minimum vehicle parking.
Existing parking may be converted to take advantage of this provision.

(Ord. No. 2018-003, § 3(Exh. 6), 10-8-2018)
