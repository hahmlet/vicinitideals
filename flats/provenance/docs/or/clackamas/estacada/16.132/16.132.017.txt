16.132.017 Notice Of Application To Governmental Agencies

Notice of the application shall be provided to any governmental agency that is entitled to notice under an intergovernmental agreement entered into with the City and any other affected agencies.
At a minimum, the City shall notify the transportation authority if different than the City of Estacada, including but not limited to ODOT, Clackamas County, Sandy Area Metro, or TriMet facilities.
The failure of another agency to respond with written comments on a pending application shall not invalidate an action or permit approval made by the City under this Code.

(Ord. No. 2018-003, § 3(Exh. 6), 10-8-2018)
