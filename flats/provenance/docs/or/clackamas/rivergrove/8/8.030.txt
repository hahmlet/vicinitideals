Section 8.030.  Procedure for Mailed Notice.
Unless otherwise required, addresses for mailed notice required by this ordinance shall be obtained from the county real property tax record.
Unless the name and address is on file with the City, a person whose name is not on the tax records at the time an application 13 filed, or other action not based on an application is begun, need not be furnished mailed notice.
Failure to receive notice shall not invalidate an action if a good faith effort was made to comply with the requirements for notice.
In addition to persons required to be given notice by this ordinance, the Commission shall provide notice to persons it has reasonable cause to believe are affected by the proposal.
