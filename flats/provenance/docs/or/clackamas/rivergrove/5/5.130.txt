Section 5.130.  Gates or Other Barriers.
Gates or other barriers shall not be used to restrict vehicular access to residential developments from any public or private street, except as specifically required or allowed by the City Council to address an operational or safety issue.
For the purpose of this Section, residential development means a residential partition or subdivision or 2 or more dwelling units on a single lot or parcel or 2 or more residential lots or parcels.
