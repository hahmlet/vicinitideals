Section 3.040.  Vacancies and Removal.
Appointments to fill vacancies shall be for the remainder of any expired term.
A member may be removed by the City Council, after hearing, for misconduct or nonperformance of duty.
A member who is absent from three (3) consecutive meetings, without good cause shown, shall be rebuttably presumed to be in nonperformance of duty and the City Council shall declare the position to be vacant unless it finds otherwise after a public hearing.
