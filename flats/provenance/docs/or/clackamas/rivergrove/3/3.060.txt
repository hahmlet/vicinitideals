Section 3.060.  Meetings.
The Commission shall meet at least once a month and shall be open to the public.
Meetings other than at regularly scheduled times may be announced at a prior meeting which announcement shall be entered in the minutes.
The chairperson upon his/her own motion, or upon the request of two or more of the Commission members, may call a special meeting previously unannounced and notice shall be given to other Commission members no later than 24 hours after such notice.
Such notice shall be posted at the City Hall and, to the extent possible under the circumstances, shall be given to the public and other interested persons.
