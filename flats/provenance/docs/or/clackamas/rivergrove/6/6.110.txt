Section 6.110.  Type III Land Division.
A land division of major significance shall be processed under a Type III procedure by the Commission.
A land division of major significance is one of the following:

(a)       A land division that ~ill create a street that is not part of a planned street; or

(b)       A land division that the Commission finds may either eliminate or make impractical the establishment of a planned street.
The applicant shall provide an ownership list containing the names and addresses of property owners within 200 feet of the property to be divided.
