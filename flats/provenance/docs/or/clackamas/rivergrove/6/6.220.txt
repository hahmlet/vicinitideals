Section 6.220.  Effective Date for Approval.
Approval for a subdivision shall become final upon the recording of the approved subdivision plat under ORS 92.120(1), and for a partition, upon the recording of the approved partition map together with any required documents with the county recorder.
Final approval constitutes the approval necessary before a development permit may be issued.
Action expressly authorized by a tentative approval may take place prior to issuance of the development permit.
