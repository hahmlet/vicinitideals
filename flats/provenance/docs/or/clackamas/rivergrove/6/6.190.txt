Section 6.190.  Submission and Review of Final Plat or Map.
A final plat or map shall be submitted and, at its next regular meeting, the Commission shall determine whether it complies with the approved tentative plan and this ordinance.
If it does not comply, the applicant shall be notified and afforded an opportunity to make corrections.· Upon compliance, the plat or map shall be signed and dated by the Commission if all other requirements for a development permit have been met
