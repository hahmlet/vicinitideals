Section 6.170.  Tentative Plan Expiration Date.
Within six months after the effective date of approval of a tentative land division plan, the final plat or map shall be submitted in the form required by Section4.040 and shall further state any modification or condition required by approval of the tentative plan.
The Commission may, upon written request, grant an extension of time for up to six months upon a written finding that the facts relied on to grant the initial approval have not significantly changed and that no other development approval will be affected.
