Section 6.300.  Conditional Use.
The Planning Commission may allow a conditional use, after a hearing conducted pursuant to the Type III land use procedure in the Rivergrove Land Development Ordinance provided the applicant complies with the relevant Application Requirements of this section and demonstrates that the proposed use satisfies the applicable conditions set forth in this ordinance and the Rivergrove Land Development Ordinance.
For the purposes of Section 6.300 through 6.340, the Planning Commission has the responsibility and authority to determine the relevance of requirements and criteria and the applicability of conditions.
