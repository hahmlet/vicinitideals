Section 6.135.  Public and Private Streets and Driveways.
Public and private streets and driveways providing access to residential developments from any public or private street shall not be gated, except as specifically required or allowed by the City Council to address an operational or safety issue.
For the purpose of this Section, residential development means a residential partition or subdivision or 2 or more dwelling units on a single lot or parcel or 2 or more residential lots or parcels.
