Section 6.070.  Authority to Grant Hardship Relief Under Type I

Procedure.  The Council may grant hardship relief under a Type I procedure if the request involves only the expansion or reduction by no more than 20 percent of one or more quantifiable provisions of yard or area standards of the Development Standards Document or comparable deviation from such additional standards set by the Commission.
The decision shall be based on the criteria in
Section 6.060.
