Section 6.228.  Public Participation.
The Commission shall provide all interested persons the opportunity to submit written recommendations and comments in advance of the hearing and this material shall be available for public inspection prior to the hearing.
At the hearing, written recommendations and other material shall be received and oral statements shall be permitted.
In addition to matters relating to compliance with applicable criteria and standards of this ordinance and with the Plan, a person may submit testimony and evidence regarding desirable policy the City should adopt.
