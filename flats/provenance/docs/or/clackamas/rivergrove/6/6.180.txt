Section 6.180.  Staged Subdivision Development.
When an application desires to record and develop subdivision plats covering portions of an approved tentative plan in stages, the Commission may authorize a time schedule for platting and otherwise developing the stages in periods of time in excess of one year but in no case shall the total time for all stages be more than five years, unless there is a resubmission of the tentative plan.
Each stage platted and developed shall comply with the plan and this ordinance and portions platted after one year may be required to be modified to comply with amendments to the plan or this ordinance and other regulations.
