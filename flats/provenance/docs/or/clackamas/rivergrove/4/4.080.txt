Section 4.080.  Action On Resubmission of Denied Application.
Upon a final determination denying an application, an applicant may take appropriate measures to cure the defect and resubmit the proposal with payment of any additional fees required.
If a previously denied application is submitted within one year, previous approvals need not be reconsidered unless the Commission

finds that changed conditions or changes in the proposal warrant reconsideration of the entire proposal.
