59.060 CONDITIONAL USES

Only the following conditional uses are allowed in this zone subject to the provisions of Chapter 60 CDC, Conditional Uses:

1. Certified child care center.

2. Community building.

3. Religious institution.

4. Senior center.

5. Public support and public safety facilities, including public parking lots.

6. Nursery.

7. Parks and open space.

8. Eating and drinking establishments.

9. Food and beverage retail sales.

10. Hotel/motel.

11. Construction sales and services.

12. Parking facilities.
(Ord. 1515, 2005; Ord. 1547, 2007; Ord. 1655 § 6, 2016; Ord. 1675 § 48, 2018; Ord. 1686 § 6, 2018; Ord. 1745 § 1 (Exh.
A), 2023)
