59.010 PURPOSE

The purpose of the mixed use/transitional zone is to provide for a transitional area between commercial and residential zones with a desirable mix of residential land uses with limited commercial land uses.
The limited commercial uses allowed in this district are selected for their compatibility with residential uses and their ability to meet the needs of the neighborhood.
Uses in this district are intended to be compatible with the design and aesthetic qualities of the adjacent neighborhood.
This zone is intended to implement the Willamette neighborhood plan as authorized by the policies set forth in the Comprehensive Plan.
(Ord. 1515, 2005; Ord. 1547, 2007; Ord. 1745 § 1 (Exh.
A), 2023)
