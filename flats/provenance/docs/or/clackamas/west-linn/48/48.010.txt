48.010 PURPOSE

The purpose of this chapter is to ensure that efficient, safe, and well-directed vehicular, bicycle, and pedestrian access, circulation, and egress are designed into development proposals.
Access management seeks to balance mobility, the need to provide efficient, safe and timely travel with the ability to allow access to individual properties.
Proper implementation of access management techniques should guarantee reduced congestion, reduced accident rates, less need for roadway widening, conservation of energy, and reduced air pollution.
(Ord. 1584, 2008; Ord. 1745 § 1 (Exh. A), 2023)
