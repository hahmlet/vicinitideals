09.010 PURPOSE

The purpose of this district is to provide for development flexibility at a range of densities where urban services are available.
The intent of the zone is to allow development at a level which recognizes and preserves natural features of the area.
This zone may also be applied to areas where public harm to wildlife habitats may result if developed at a higher density.
(Ord. 1308, 1991; Ord. 1745 § 1 (Exh. A), 2023)
