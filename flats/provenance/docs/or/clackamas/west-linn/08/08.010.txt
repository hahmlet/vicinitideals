08.010 PURPOSE

The purpose of this district is to provide for development where there are some available services, but the services will not support the development of land at more urban densities.
The intent of the zone is to allow development at a level based on the existing service level, but in a manner which does not adversely impact environmentally sensitive areas.
This zone may also be applied to areas where public harm to wildlife habitats may result if developed at a higher density.
(Ord. 1745 § 1 (Exh.
A), 2023)
