22.090 DIMENSIONAL REQUIREMENTS, CONDITIONAL USES

Except as may otherwise be established by CDC 22.070, the appropriate lot or parcel size for a conditional use shall be determined by the approval authority at the time of consideration of the application based upon the criteria set forth in CDC 60.070(A) and (B).
(Ord. 1636 § 18, 2014; Ord. 1745 § 1 (Exh. A), 2023)
