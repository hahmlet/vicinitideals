43.030 ADMINISTRATION

The Planning Director shall apply the standards of this chapter during the administrative review of building permits.
No notice is required.
In the event that an individual or other party wants to appeal the Planning Director’s decision relative to this chapter, they may appeal the decision to the City Council within 14 days of the final decision per CDC 99.140.
For the purpose of determining the date of the final decision it shall be the Community Development Department’s stamped approval date on the plans.
(Ord. 1538, 2006; Ord. 1621 § 25, 2014 ; Ord. 1745 § 1 (Exh. A), 2023)
