43.020 APPLICABILITY

These provisions shall apply to all new single-family attached and detached construction and additions that increase the area of a side yard building elevation by 700 square feet or more, except designated historic resources.
(Ord. 1538, 2006; Ord. 1614 § 9, 2013; Ord. 1745 § 1 (Exh. A), 2023)
