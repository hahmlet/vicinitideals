43.010 PURPOSE

New homes, both infill and in new subdivisions, particularly new homes around the perimeter of the new subdivision, need to be compatible with adjacent existing homes, especially when the new house is bigger than the existing one.
To this end, transitions shall be required to avoid a monolithic and overbearing sidewall.
(Ord. 1538, 2006 ; Ord. 1745 § 1 (Exh. A), 2023)
