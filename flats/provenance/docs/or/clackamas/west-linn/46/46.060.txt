46.060 STORAGE IN PARKING AND LOADING AREAS PROHIBITED

Parking spaces shall be available for the parking of passenger automobiles of residents, customers, patrons and employees only, and the parking spaces shall not be used for storage of vehicles or materials or for the parking of trucks connected with the business or use with the exception of small (under one-ton) delivery trucks or cars.
(Ord. 1745 § 1 (Exh.
A), 2023; Ord. 1754 § 1 (Exh.
A), 2024)
