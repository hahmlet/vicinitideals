46.120 DRIVEWAYS REQUIRED ON SITE

Any school or other meeting place which is designed to accommodate more than 25 people at one time shall provide a 15-foot-wide driveway designed for continuous forward flow of passenger vehicles for the purpose of loading and unloading passengers.
Depending on functional requirements, the width may be increased with Planning Director approval.
(Ord. 1745 § 1 (Exh. A), 2023)
