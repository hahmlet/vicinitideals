TDC 65.200.  Use Categories.
(1)  Use Categories. Table 65-1 lists use categories Permitted Outright (P) or Conditionally Permitted (C) in the BCE zone.
Use categories may also be designated as Limited (L) and subject to the limitations listed in Table 65-1 and restrictions identified in TDC 65.210.
Limitations may restrict the specific type of use, location, size, or other characteristics of the use category.
Use categories which are not listed are prohibited within the zone, except for uses which are found by the City Manager or appointee to be of a similar character and to meet the purpose of this zone, as provided in TDC 31.070.
