TDC 58.400  Use Categories in the CO Zone.
(1)  Modifications to Base Zone Use Regulations. Some of the uses permitted in the CO zone are
modified in the Central Tualatin Overlay zone. Table 58-4 lists use categories that are modified in the
overlay zone as Permitted Outright (P), Conditionally Permitted (C), or Prohibited (N). Use catego-
ries may also be designated as Limited (L) and subject to the limitations listed in Table 58-4. Use
categories not listed in Table 58-4 are regulated as specified in the CO zone see TDC Chapter 50.
(2)  Sub-Districts. Block 1 is the only sub-district in the overlay zone. The modifications to use
regulations in Table 58-4 apply exclusively to Block 1.
