TDC 31.105.  Interpretation, Conflict and Rules of Construction.
(1)  The provisions of this Chapter are minimum requirements.
(2)  Unless a particular provision of the Tualatin Development Code provides otherwise, where two
or more provisions of the Tualatin Development Code conflict, the provisions must be read in
conjunction to give both meaning, unless only one can be given meaning in which case the provision
imposing the more restrictive or higher standard applies.
(3)  Unless a particular provision of the Tualatin Development Code provides otherwise, the rules of
statutory construction under ORS Chapter 174, as interpreted by Oregon courts, apply to the
Tualatin Development Code.
(Ord. 1414-18, 12-10-18)
