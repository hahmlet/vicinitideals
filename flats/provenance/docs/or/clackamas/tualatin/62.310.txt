TDC 62.310.  Additional Development Standards.
(1)  Industrial Master Plan. Minimum lot size, setbacks, maximum height, and other development
standards may be modified by submittal of an Industrial Master Plan application. See TDC 33.050.
(2)  Spur Rail Tracks. Spur rail tracks are not permitted within 200 feet of an adjacent residential
district.
