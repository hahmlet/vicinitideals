TDC 64.200.  Use Categories.
(1)  Use Categories. Table 64-1 lists use categories Permitted Outright (P) or Conditionally Permitted (C) in the MBP zone.
Use categories may also be designated as Limited (L) and subject to the limitations listed in Table 64-1 and restrictions identified in TDC 64.210.
Limitations may restrict the specific type of use, location, size, or other characteristics of the use category.
Use categories which are not listed are prohibited within the zone, except for uses which are found by the City Manager or appointee to be of a similar character and to meet the purpose of this zone, as provided in TDC 31.070.
(2)  Overlay Zones. Additional uses may be allowed in a particular overlay zone.
See the overlay zone Chapters for additional uses.
