TDC 73C.060.  Bicycle and Transit Facility Conversion.
Any portion of existing off-street parking areas may be redeveloped as a bicycle-oriented or
transit-oriented facility including bicycle parking, bus stops and pullouts, bus shelters and park and
ride stations, and similar facilities. Conversion to such uses is reviewed through the Architectural
Review process.
(Ord. No. 1486-24, § 13, 6-10-24)
