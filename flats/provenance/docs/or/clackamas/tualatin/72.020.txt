TDC 72.020. Location of Greenways and Natural Areas.
(1) The designated significant natural resources are the Greenways and Natural Areas on Map
72-1, which shows the general location of the NRPO District.
(2) Lands in the Wetland Protection District (WPD) are subject to Chapter 71, and other applicable
regulations, but not Chapter 72.
