TDC 72.130. Floodplain District.
In cases where land within the NRPO District is also within the Floodplain District, Chapter 70, any
development permitted by TDC 72.060 shall be subject to the provisions of Chapter 70.
(Ord. 933-94, § 42, 11-28-94; Ord. 947-95, § 5, 7-24-95; Ord. 979-97, § 46, 7-14-97)
