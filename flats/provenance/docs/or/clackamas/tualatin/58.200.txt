TDC 58.200  Use Categories in the CC Zone.
(1)  Modifications to Base Zone Use Regulations. Some of the uses permitted in the CC zone are
modified in the Central Tualatin Overlay zone. Table 58-1 lists use categories that are modified in the
overlay zone as Permitted Outright (P), Conditionally Permitted (C), or Prohibited (N). Use catego-
ries may also be designated as Limited (L) and subject to the limitations listed in Table 58-1 and
restrictions identified in TDC 58.210. Use categories not listed in Table 58-1 are regulated as
specified in the CC zone see TDC Chapter 53.
(2)  Sub-Districts. Modifications to use regulations may vary by the sub-district within the overlay
zone. There are three sub-districts within the CC zone in the Central Tualatin Overlay Zone. These
sub-districts are defined by the block numbers listed below, except as otherwise noted.
(a)  Residential Sub-District. Blocks 2, 3, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 20, 22 and 23.
(b)  Commercial Sub-District. Block 30.
