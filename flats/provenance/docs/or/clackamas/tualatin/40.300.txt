TDC 40.300.  Development Standards.
(1)  Development standards in the RL zone are listed in Table 40-3. Additional standards may apply
to some uses and situations, see TDC 40.310. The standards in Table 40-3 may be modified for
flexible lot subdivisions as provided in TDC 36.410 and for greenway and natural area dedications
as provided in TDC 36.420.
(2)  Exceptions. Existing nonconforming situations may be developed according to the provisions
of TDC Chapter 35.
