TDC 58.800  Central Tualatin Overlay Development Standards.
(1)  Development standards in the Central Tualatin Overlay Zone are listed in Table 58-7 by zone and by block.
Where no standard is listed, the standards of the base zone apply.
(2)  Exceptions. Existing nonconforming situations may be developed according to the provisions of TDC Chapter 35.
