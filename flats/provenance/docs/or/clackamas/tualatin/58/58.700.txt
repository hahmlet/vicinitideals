TDC 58.700  Use Categories in the RH-HR Zone.
Within the Central Tualatin Overlay Zone, the uses permitted in the RH-HR zone are not modified.
The RH-HR zone allows for high-density development in the Central Tualatin Overlay Zone.
Some development standards, including density and minimum lot size, for sites within the RH-HR zone in the Central Tualatin Overlay are subject to TDC 58.800.
