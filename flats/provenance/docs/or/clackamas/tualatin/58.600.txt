TDC 58.600  Use Categories in the RH Zone.
(1)  Modifications to Base Zone Use Regulations. Within the Central Tualatin Overlay Zone, the
uses permitted in the RH zone may be mixed with the uses permitted in the CC zone. Development
standards for sites within the RH zone in the Central Tualatin Overlay are subject to TDC 58.800.
(2)  Sub-Districts. Blocks 25 and 4 are the only sub-districts in the overlay zone.
(Ord. No. 1450-20, § 35, 12-14-20)
