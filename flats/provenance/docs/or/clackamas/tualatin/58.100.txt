TDC 58.100  Purpose.
The overall goal of the Central Tualatin Overlay Zone is to strengthen the social and economic
development of central Tualatin; encourage and facilitate land uses, private and public, that result in
activity during all business hours, evenings, nights, and weekends; and to encourage indoor and
outdoor uses. The overlay zone regulations are intended to ensure development contributes
towards these goals.
(Ord. No. 1450-20, § 29, 12-14-20)
