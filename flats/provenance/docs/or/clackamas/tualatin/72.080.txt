TDC 72.080. Shift of Density for Residential Development Adjacent to Greenways or Natural
Areas.
(1) A shift of density may be allowed in accordance with TDC 41.150 (RML District), 42.150 (RMH
District), 43.180 (RH District) and 44.160 (RH/HR District).
(2) Small lots may be allowed in subdivisions and partitions in accordance with TDC 40.055 (RL
District).
(Ord. 979-97, § 40, 7-14-97; Ord. 1136-03, 4-28-03)
