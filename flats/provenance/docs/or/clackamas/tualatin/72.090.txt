TDC 72.090. Reduction in Setback Requirements.
When a property owner in a IN, CO, CR, CO/MR, ML, or MG Planning District dedicates to the City
land in the NRPO District, a bikeway or pedestrian path facility, or a vegetated corridor located within
or adjacent to the NRPO District, the minimum front yard setback may be reduced through the AR
process as provided in Chapters 50, 51, 52, 55, 60, and 61.
(Ord. 721-87, § 3, 5-26-87; Ord. 933-94, § 38, 11-28-94; Ord. 947-95, § 5, 7-24-95; Ord. 979-97,
§ 42, 7-14-97; Ord. 1216-06, 7-24-06; Ord. 1427-19, § 37, 11-25-19)
