TDC 72.120. Wetlands Protection District.
In cases where land within the NRPO District is also within the Wetlands Protection District, Chapter
71, any development permitted by TDC 72.060 shall be subject to the provisions of Chapter 71.
(Ord. 933-94, § 41, 11-28-94; Ord. 947-95, § 5, 7-24-95; Ord. 979-97, § 45, 7-14-97)
