407.03 USES PERMITTED

Uses permitted in the AG/F District are those allowed in the EFU District, subject to Section 401, Exclusive Farm Use District (EFU), and those allowed in the TBR District, subject to Section 406, Timber District (TBR).
However, the following limitations apply.

A. Dwellings are subject to the applicable provisions of Section 401 if the predominant use of the subject tract was agriculture on January 1, 1993.
Dwellings are subject to the applicable provisions of Section 406 if the predominant use of the subject tract was forestry on January 1, 1993.

B. If a use, other than a dwelling, is allowed by both Section 401 and Section 406, the use shall comply with the applicable standards of both sections.
If there is a conflict between a standard in Section 401 and a standard in Section 406, to the extent that it is not possible for a use to comply with both, the most restrictive standard shall apply.
