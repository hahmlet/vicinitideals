510.04 Dimensional Standards

Dimensional standards applicable in the urban commercial and mixed-use zoning districts are listed in Table 510-2, Dimensional Standards in the Urban Commercial and Mixed-Use Zoning Districts.
Modifications to the standards of Table 510-2 are established by Sections 800, Special Use Requirements; 903, Setback Exceptions; 904, Height Exceptions; 1012, Lot Size and Density; 1107, Property Line Adjustments; and 1205, Variances.
As used in Table 510-2, numbers in superscript correspond to the notes that follow Table 510-2.
