Section 4.199.60. Major Additions or Modifications to Pre-Existing Sites.
(01.) Major Additions.
If a major addition occurs on a property, all of the luminaires on the site shall comply with the requirements of this Section.
For purposes of this sub-section, the following are considered to be major additions:
A. Additions of 50 percent or more in terms of additional dwelling units, gross floor area, seating capacity, or parking spaces, either with a single addition or with cumulative additions after July 2, 2008.
B. Modification or replacement of 50 percent or more of the outdoor lighting luminaries' within a five-year timeframe existing as of July 2, 2008.
