Section 4.630.10. Display of Permit; Inspection.
The Tree Removal Permit grantee shall conspicuously display the permit on-site.
The permit grantee shall display the permit continuously while trees are being removed or replaced or while activities authorized under the permit are performed.
The permit grantee shall allow City representatives to enter and inspect the premises at any reasonable time, and failure to allow inspection shall constitute a violation of this subchapter.
