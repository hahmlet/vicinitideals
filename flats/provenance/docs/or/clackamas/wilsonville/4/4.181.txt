Section 4.181. Exceptions and Modifications—Height Limits.
Except as stipulated in Sections 4.800 through 4.804, height limitations specified elsewhere in this Code shall not apply to barns, silos or other farm buildings or structures on farms; to church spires, belfries, cupolas; and domes; monuments; water towers; windmills; chimneys; smokestacks; fire and hose towers; above-ground electric transmission, distribution, communication and signal lines, towers and poles; and properly screened mechanical and elevator structures.
(Ord. No. 856, 2-7-2022)
