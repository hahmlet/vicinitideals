Section 4.175.  Public Safety and Crime Prevention.
(.01) All developments shall be designed to deter crime and insure public safety.
(.02) Addressing and directional signing shall be designed to assure identification of all buildings and structures by emergency response personnel, as well as the general public.
(.03) Areas vulnerable to crime shall be designed to allow surveillance.
Parking and loading areas shall be designed for access by police in the course of routine patrol duties.
(.04) Exterior lighting shall be designed and oriented to discourage crime.
