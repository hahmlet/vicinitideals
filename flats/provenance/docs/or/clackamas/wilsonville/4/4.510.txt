Section 4.510. Conditional Use Permit—Findings In Support of Granting.
(.01) A Greenway Conditional Use Permit may be granted by the Development Review Board upon making the findings required in Section 4.184 (Conditional Use Permits) and the following additional findings:
A. That to the greatest extent possible, the maximum possible landscape area, open space or vegetation between the activity and the river are provided,
B. That to the greatest extent possible, necessary public access in accordance with the Comprehensive Plan will be provided to and along the River by appropriate legal means.
C. That the change of use, intensification of use, or development complies with this Code, all other applicable City Ordinances, the Comprehensive Plan, and the Oregon State Parks and Recreation Department Greenway Plan.
