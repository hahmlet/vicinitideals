Section 4.809.  Preexisting WCF.
WCF that lawfully existed prior to the adoption of this Chapter shall be allowed to continue their use as they presently exist.
This Code does not make lawful any WCF that are not fully approved on the date the ordinance codified in this Code is adopted and those pending WCF will be required to meet the requirements of this Code.
Routine maintenance shall be permitted on such lawful preexisting WCF .
Lawfully existing WCF may be replaced as long as the replacement is in the exact location of the WCF being replaced and is of a construction type identical in height, width, weight, lighting, and painting.
Any changes or modifications to a replacement WCF shall not be considered routine maintenance, shall be treated as new construction, and shall comply with the requirements of this Chapter.
(Ord. No. 831, 1-24-2019; Ord. No. 882, § 2(Exh. A), 10-2-2023)
