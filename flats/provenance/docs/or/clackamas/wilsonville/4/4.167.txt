Section 4.167. General Regulations—Access, Ingress and Egress.
(.01) Each access onto streets or private drives shall be at defined points as approved by the City and shall be consistent with the public's health, safety and general welfare.
Such defined points of access shall be approved at the time of issuance of a building permit if not previously determined in the development permit.
(Ord. No. 682, 9-9-2010)
