Section 4.810.  Ancillary Facilities.
Unenclosed storage of materials is prohibited.
Other building facilities, including offices, vehicle storage areas, or other similar uses not necessary for transmission or relay functions, are prohibited unless a separate land use application for such is submitted and approved.
(Ord. No. 831, 1-24-2019; Ord. No. 882, § 2(Exh. A), 10-2-2023)
