Section 4.508. Conditional Use Permit—Uses Permitted Conditionally .
(.01) The following uses may be allowed within the Willamette River Greenway Boundaries subject to a Conditional Use Permit by the Development Review Board:
A. All uses permitted in the underlying zone which are not listed as permitted uses in Section 4.506.
B. All uses which are classified as intensification of use, change of use or development, other than tree removal, which shall be governed by the provisions of Section 4.600.
