Section 4.000.  Administration—Purpose and Title.
(.01) Chapter 4 of this Code is enacted for the purpose of promoting the general public welfare by ensuring procedural due process in the administration and enforcement of the City's Comprehensive Plan, Zoning, Design Review, Land Division, and Development Standards.
(.02) This ordinance shall be known as the Planning and Land Development Ordinance.
It shall include those ordinances familiarly referred to as the Zoning Ordinance, Subdivision Ordinance, Sign Ordinance, Tree Preservation Ordinance, Development Code, etc.
