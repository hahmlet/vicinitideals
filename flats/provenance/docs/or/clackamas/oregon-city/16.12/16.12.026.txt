16.12.026 Same -- Alle y s.
Alleys with public access easements on private prop ­ erty shall be provided in the Park Place and South End con­ cept plan areas for the following districts R- 5, R- 3.5,
R-2, MUC-1, MUC-2 and NC zones unless other permanent pro ­ visions for private access to off-street parking and load­ ing facilities are approved by the decision maker.
All al­ leys intended to provide access for emergency vehicles shall be a minimum width of twenty feet.
The corners of al­ ley intersections shall have a radius of not less than ten feet and shall conform to standards approved by the city engineer.
Access easements and maintenance agreements shall be recorded on affected properties.
(Ord. No. 18 - 1009, § l(Exh. A), 7-3-2019; Ord. No. 19-1008,
§ l(Exh. A), 12-18-2019)
