16.12.008 Definitions.
Whenever the words or terms and their derivatives are used in this chapter, they shall have the meaning herein ascribed to them as described in OCMC 17.04, unless the context dictates application of a different meaning.
(Ord. No. 18-1009, § l(Exh. A), 7-3-2019; Ord. No. 19-1008,
§ l(Exh. A), 12-18-2019)
