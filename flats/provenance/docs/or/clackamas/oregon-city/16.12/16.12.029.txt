16.12.029 Excavations--Restoration of pavement.
Whenever any excavation shall have been made in any pavement or other street improvement on any street or alley in the city for any purpose whatsoever under the permit granted by the engineer, it shall be the duty of the person making the excavation to restore the pavement in accordance with the city of Oregon City Public Works Pavement Cut
Standards in effect at the time the permit is granted.
The city commission may adopt and modify the city of Oregon
City Public Works Pavement Cut Standards by resolution as necessary to implement the requirements of this chapter.
(Ord. No. 18-1009, § l(Exh. A), 7-3-2019; Ord. No. 19-1008,
§ l(Exh. A), 12-18-2019)
