§ 16.67.020. Legislative amendments.

Legislative amendments are policy decisions made by City Council.
Except in the case of expedited annexation, they are reviewed using the Type IV procedure in Section 16.61.050 and shall conform to the provisions in Section 16.67.030(C).

(Ord. 389 § 1(Exh.
A), 2009; Ord. 439 § 1(Exh.
A), 2013; Ord. 582, 3/19/2024)
