§ 16.42.020. Applicability.

This chapter shall apply to all land divisions and developments subject to site design review.
Accessory dwelling units, single-family detached, single-family attached dwellings, duplexes, triplexes, quadplexes, or cottage clusters are not subject to Section 16.42.030(D), (F), (G), (H), (J) and (K).

(Ord. 389 § 1, 2009; Ord. 582, 3/19/2024)
