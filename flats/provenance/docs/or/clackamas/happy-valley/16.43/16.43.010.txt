§ 16.43.010. Purpose.

The intent of these regulations is to provide adequate capacity and appropriate location and design of on-site parking and loading areas as well as adequate access to such areas.
The parking requirements are intended to provide sufficient parking in close proximity for residents, customers, and/or employees of various land uses.
These regulations apply to both motorized vehicles (hereinafter referred to as vehicles) and bicycles.

(Ord. 389 § 1(Exh. A), 2009)
