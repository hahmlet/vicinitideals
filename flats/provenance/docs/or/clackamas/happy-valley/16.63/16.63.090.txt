§ 16.63.090. Public improvements required.

Before City approval is certified on the final plat, all required public improvements shall be installed, inspected, and approved.
Alternatively, if 80% of the required public improvements are complete, the subdivider/partitioner shall provide a performance guarantee for the incomplete work, in accordance with Section 16.63.100.

(Ord. 389 § 1(Exh. A), 2009; Ord. 433 § 1, 2013; Ord. 439 § 1(Exh. B), 2013)
