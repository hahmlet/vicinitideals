§ 19.202.3. Measuring Tree Diameter.

A.

Existing Trees

Existing trees are measured at a height 4.5 ft above the mean ground level at the base of the tree, also sometimes referred to as "diameter at breast height."
Trees on slopes are measured from the ground level on the lower side of the tree.
If a tree splits into multiple trunks below 4.5 ft above ground level, the diameter is measured at its most narrow point below the split.

B.

New Trees

New trees are measured in caliper inches, which is the diameter of the trunk 6 in above the mean ground level at the base of the tree.
