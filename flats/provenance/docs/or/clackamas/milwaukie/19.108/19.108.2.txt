§ 19.108.2. Special Area Maps.

The special areas described in Subsection 19.108.1 are not displayed on the City's Zon-ing Map.
They are shown on specific administrative maps.

Natural resources are displayed on the Natural Resource (NR) Administrative Map, which is adopted by reference.
Provisions for administering the NR Administrative Map are established in Subsection 19.402.15.
