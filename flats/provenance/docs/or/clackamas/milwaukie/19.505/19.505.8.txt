§ 19.505.8. Building Orientation to Transit.

The following requirement applies to all new commercial, office, mixed-use, and institutional development within 500 ft of an existing or planned transit route measured along the public sidewalk that provides direct access to the transit route:

New buildings shall have their primary orientation toward a transit street or, if not adjacent to a transit street, a public right-of-way which leads to a transit street.
The primary building entrance shall be visible from the street and shall be directly accessible from a sidewalk connected to the public right-of-way.
A building may have more than one entrance.
If the development has frontage on more than one transit street, the primary building entrance may be oriented to either street or to the corner.
