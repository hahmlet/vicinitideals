§ 19.301.2. Allowed Uses in Moderate Density Residential Zones.

Uses allowed, either allowed by right or conditionally, in the moderate density residential zones are listed in Table 19.301.2 below.
Similar uses not listed in the table may be allowed through a Director's determination pursuant to Section 19.903.
Notes and/or cross references to other applicable code sections are listed in the "Standards/Additional Provisions" column.

See Section 19.201 Definitions for specific descriptions of the uses listed in the table.
