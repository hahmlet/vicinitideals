§ 19.401.7. Setbacks.

On a case-by-case basis, uses that are not water-dependent or water-related shall be evaluated according to criteria of Subsection 19.401.6 above so that they are directed away from the river.
Existing and proposed uses that are water-dependent and water-oriented may be permitted near or at the water's edge subject to review of criteria in Subsection 19.401.6 above.
