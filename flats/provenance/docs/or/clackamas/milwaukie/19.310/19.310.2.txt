§ 19.310.2. Uses.

A.

Permitted Uses.

Uses allowed outright are listed in Table 19.310.2 with a "P".
These uses are allowed if they comply with the development and design standards and other regulations of this title.

B.

Limited Uses.

Uses listed in Table 19.310.2 as "L" are permitted only as limited uses in conformance with Section 19.310.3.

C.

Community Service Uses.

Uses listed in Table 19.310.2 as "CSU" are permitted only as community service uses in conformance with Section 19.904.

D.

Conditional Uses.

Uses listed in Table 19.310.2 as "CU" are permitted only as conditional uses in conformance with Section 19.905.

E.

Nonconforming Uses, Structures, and Development.

Existing structures and uses that do not meet the standards of this zone may continue in existence.
Except where otherwise stated in this section, the Provisions of Chapter 19.800 Nonconforming Uses and Development apply.

F.

Prohibited Uses.

Uses not listed in Table 19.310.2 or determined to be similar pursuant to Subsection 19.310.2H below, are prohibited.

G.

Similar Uses.

The Planning Manager, through a Type I review, may determine that a use that is not listed is considered similar to an example use listed in Table 19.310.2.
The unlisted use will be subject to the standards applicable to the similar example use.

This category comprises establishments engaged in the mechanical, physical, or chemical transformation of materials, substances, or components into new products, including the assembly of component parts.

Section 19.905
Conditional Uses
Trade schools and training facilities[1]
This category comprises establishments whose primary purpose is to provide training for industrial needs and job specific certification.
Examples include: electronic equipment repair training, truck-driving school, welding school, training for repair of industrial machinery, job skills training classrooms, and other industrial/employment skills training.
P
Wholesale trade, warehousing, and distribution
This category comprises establishments engaged in selling and/or distributing merchandise to retailers; to industrial, commercial, or professional business users; or to other wholesalers, generally without transformation, and rendering services incidental to the sale of merchandise.
Wholesalers sell or distribute merchandise exclusively to other businesses, not the general public, and normally operate from a warehouse or office and are not intended for walk-in traffic.
Examples include: operating warehousing and storage facilities for general merchandise, refrigerated goods, and other products and materials that have been manufactured and are generally being stored in anticipation of delivery to final customer.
Includes fleet parking.
Fulfillment centers engaged in selling and/or distributing merchandise to individual consumers are not included in this category.
