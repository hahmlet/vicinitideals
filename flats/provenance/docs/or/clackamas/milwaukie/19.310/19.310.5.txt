§ 19.310.5. Detailed Development Standards.

The following detailed development standards describe additional allowances, restrictions, and exemptions related to the development standards of Table 19.310.4.

A.

Screening of Outdoor Uses.

Outdoor uses must be screened as follows:

1.

Outside storage is permitted in side or rear yards.
Outside storage adjacent to International Way, Freeman Way, 37th Ave., Lake Road, or Hwy. 224 is prohibited.

2.

All outdoor storage areas must be screened from adjacent properties and public streets by a 6-ft-high sight-obscuring fence or wall or by the use of vegetation.
Vegetation used to screen outdoor storage areas must be of such species, number, and spacing to provide the required screening within one year after planting.

3.

All plantings used to screen outdoor uses must be maintained on an ongoing basis and shall be replaced if vegetation is diseased, dying, or dead.

4.

All roof-mounted mechanical equipment, except solar collection apparatus, must be screened or masked from view from adjacent major streets (Hwy 224, International Way, Harmony Road, and 37th Avenue).

B.

Nuisances.

The use must not be of a type or intensity that produces dust, odor, smoke, fumes, noise, glare, heat, vibrations, or other similar impacts that are incompatible with other uses allowed in this zone.
In addition, the use must not produce off-site impacts that create nuisances as defined by the Oregon Department of Environmental Quality (DEQ) or the noise control standards established in Chapter 8.08.
