§ 19.310.3. Limited Uses.

Limited uses are subject to the following limitations and conditions:

A.

Limited uses may occupy up to a maximum of 25% of the square footage of a building.
A limited use that is to be located in a building and exceeds 25% of the building's square footage must be reviewed as a conditional use;

B.

The maximum floor area for a limited use is 4,000 sq ft;

C.

Limited uses must comply with all other standards in Section 19.310.
