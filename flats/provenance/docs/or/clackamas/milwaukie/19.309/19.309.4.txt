§ 19.309.4. Additional Development Standards.

The following development standards apply to all uses in the M Zone.

A.

Outdoor Storage.

A permitted use may require outside storage areas.
These storage areas must be screened with a 6-ft high sight-obscuring fence or dense plantings from any adjoining residential uses or public streets.
Vegetation used to screen outdoor storage areas must be of such species, number, and spacing to provide the required screening within one year after planting.
All plantings used to screen outdoor uses must be maintained on an ongoing basis and must be replaced if vegetation is diseased, dying, or dead.

B.

Transition area.

Industrial development within 120 ft of areas zoned for residential uses is subject to Type II review per Section 19.906 Development Review.
The review authority may attach conditions to reduce any potentially adverse impacts to residential properties.
The following characteristics will be considered:

1.

Lighting – On-site lighting should not extend beyond the property line, nor should it point towards the residential zone.

2.

Hours of operation – Operating characteristics between 10:00 p.m. and 7:00 a.m. should be reviewed to mitigate impacts.

3.

Delivery and shipping – To the extent practicable, delivery and shipping doors should be oriented away from the residential zone.

4.

Distance to residential zone boundary – The potential for impacts and mitigation should include consideration of the yard size between the building and the residential zone boundary.

C.

Uses must not be of a type or intensity that produces dust, odor, smoke, fumes, noise, glare, heat, vibrations, or other similar impacts that are incompatible with other uses allowed in this zone.
In addition, the use must not produce off-site impacts that create nuisances as defined by the Oregon Department of Environmental Quality (D.E.Q.) or the noise control standards established in Chapter 8.08.
