§ 19.304.4. Development Standards.

In the downtown zones, the development standards in Table 19.304.4 apply.
Notes and/or cross references to other applicable code sections are listed in the "standards/Additional Provisions" column.
Additional standards are provided in Subsection 19.304.5.

See Sections 19.201 Definitions and 19.202 Measurements for specific descriptions of standards and measurements listed in the table.

The development standards address several issues of particular importance to maintaining the appropriate character for the downtown.
Table 19.304.4 summarizes some of the development standards that apply downtown.
Development standards are presented in full in Subsection 19.304.5.
