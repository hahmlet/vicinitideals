§ 19.107.7. Zoning of Public Right-of-Way.

The zones applied to the public rights-of-way within the City boundaries as shown on the Zoning Map do not directly regulate the improvements or structures that are allowed in these rights-of-way.
Improvements and structures in public rights-of-way are regulated by other rules, regulations, and ordinances maintained by the City and other road authorities, such as Chapter 19.700, Public Works Standards, and the Transportation System Plan.
