§ 19.506.4. Siting Standards.

Manufactured homes are allowed by right in any zone that allows single detached dwellings by right.
Manufactured homes placed on individual lots must meet the design standards in Subsection 19.505.1 and the following standards:

A.

The unit must comply with the definition for manufactured home as identified in this section.

B.

If provided, the unit must comply with single-family parking and paving standards as described in Chapter 19.600.
