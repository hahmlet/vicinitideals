§ 19.506.3. Definition.

For the purposes of this section, the following definition shall apply:

"Manufactured home" means a single-family residential structure as defined in ORS 446.003(21)(a) which includes a Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) label certifying that the structure is constructed in accordance with the Manufactured Housing Construction and Safety Standards of 1974 (42 U.S.C.
Section 5401 et seq.) as amended on August 22, 1981.
