§ 19.405.3. Procedures.

The following procedures shall govern the application of L-F Zones:

A.

An L-F Zone may be established, altered, or abolished subject to the provisions of Section 19.902.

B.

An L-F Zone may be established in combination with other regular zones.
An area approved as an L-F Zone shall be identified on the Zoning Map or map amendments with the letters "L-F" in addition to the abbreviated designation of the existing zoning.
