§ 19.405.1. Purpose.

The purpose of the L-F Zone is to minimize hazards related to aircraft landing facilities.
Special height limitations or other restrictions in addition to those already embodied in this chapter may be necessary in order to prevent the establishment of obstructions to the air space required by aircraft in landing and taking off from airports, helicopter pads or other landing fields.
Furthermore, establishment of any new aircraft landing facility should be undertaken only after an evaluation has been made of its effects on the safety, welfare, and property values of owners or occupants of nearby property.
The L-F Zone is a superimposed zone applied in combination with existing regular zones.
