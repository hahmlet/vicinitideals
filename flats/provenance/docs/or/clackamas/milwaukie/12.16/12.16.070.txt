§ 12.16.070. VIOLATION PENALTY.

Any person, firm, or corporation violating any of the provisions of this chapter, or causing, permitting, or suffering the same to be done, shall be fined not more than two hundred fifty dollars ($250.00).
Each such person, firm, or corporation shall be deemed guilty of a separate offense for each and every day or portion thereof during which any violation of any of the provisions of this chapter is committed, continued, or permitted.

(Ord. 2004 § 1, 2009; Ord. 2236 § 2, 2023)
