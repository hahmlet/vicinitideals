§ 19.311.16. Expiration of Planned Development Zone.

If, within12 months of its effective date, substantial construction or development in the PD Zone has not commenced in compliance with the approved final development plan and program and schedule for stage completion, the Planning Commission may initiate a review of the PD Zone and hold a public hearing to determine whether its continuation in whole or in part is in the public interest.
Notification and hearing shall be in accordance with Section 19.1007.
If found not to be, the Planning Commission shall recommend to the City Council that the PD Zone be removed by appropriate amendment to the Zoning Map and property changed back to original zoning.

In the case of phased development, as governed by Subsection 19.311.17, this provision applies to the first phase of the development.
