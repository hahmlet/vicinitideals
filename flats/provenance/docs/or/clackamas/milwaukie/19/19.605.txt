§ 19.605. Vehicle Parking Quantity Requirements

The purpose of Section 19.605 is to ensure that development does not provide excessive vehicle parking based on their estimated parking demand.
Subsection 19.605.1 establishes parking ratios for common land uses, and Subsection 19.605.3 allows certain exemptions and reductions to these ratios based on location or on-site amenities.
Subsection 19.605.4 details requirements for installing electric vehicle (EV) charging infrastructure in new multi-unit, mixed-use, commercial, and industrial developments.
Modifications to the established parking ratios and determinations of parking requirements for unique land uses are allowed with discretionary review per Subsection 19.605.2.
