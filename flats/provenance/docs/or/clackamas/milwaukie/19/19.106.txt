§ 19.106. Compliance

Development may occur, a lot may be used, and a structure or part of a structure may be constructed, reconstructed, altered, occupied, or used only as this title permits.
Where this title imposes greater restrictions than those imposed or required by other rules, regulations, or ordinances, the provisions of this title shall control.
Where comparable provisions of this title are in conflict, the provisions that are the most restrictive shall control.

(Ord. 2025 § 2, 2011)
