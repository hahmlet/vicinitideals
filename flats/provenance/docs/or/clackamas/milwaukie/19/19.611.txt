§ 19.611. Parking Structures

The purpose of Section 19.611 is to regulate the design and location of structured parking, and to provide appropriate incentives for the provision of structured parking.
Structured parking is allowed to accommodate parking for a specific use, or as a parking facility that is a use by itself.

F), 2015; Ord. 2218, 2022; Ord. 2243, 5/21/2024)
