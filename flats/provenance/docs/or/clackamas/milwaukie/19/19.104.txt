§ 19.104. Interpretation

The Planning Director shall have the authority and responsibility for interpreting all terms, provisions, and requirements of this title.
Unless specifically defined in Section 19.201, words or phrases used in this title are intended to be interpreted with the meaning they have in common usage.
Requests for interpretations may be made as described and provided for in Section 19.903.

(Ord. 2025 § 2, 2011)
