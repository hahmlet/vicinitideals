§ 19.510. Green Building Standards

Green building is the practice of creating structures and using processes that are environmentally responsible and resource-efficient throughout a building's life cycle from siting to design, construction, operation, maintenance, renovation, and deconstruction.
For the purposes of height bonuses and/or meeting the local criteria for the Milwaukie Vertical Housing Development Zone Program (MMC Chapter 3.65), a green building shall be defined as a building that will achieve certification or similar approval documentation, as applicable, at any level of one of the following programs:

1.

Living Building Challenge;

2.

LEED;

3.

Earth Advantage;

4.

Passive House;

5.

Enterprise Green Communities; or

6.

Energy Trust of Oregon's New Buildings program confirming participation in the Path to Net Zero program offering.

Height bonus eligibility shall be verified at the time of building permit submittal and shall be contingent upon a green building certification submittal.
The height bonus may be binding under a development agreement and height bonus awards may be revoked, and/or other permits or approvals may be withheld, if the project fails to achieve the required energy reduction and/or certification.
