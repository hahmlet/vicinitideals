16.04.190 Dwelling, single-family.

Single-family dwelling means a detached building containing one dwelling unit. Manufactured homes shall not be considered to be single-family dwellings for the purposes of this chapter unless found to meet all city building, mechanical, electrical and other construction codes applicable to conventional units built on the site. (Ord. 740 section 10.1.20 (B)([part], 1984; Ord. 1514, 2019)
