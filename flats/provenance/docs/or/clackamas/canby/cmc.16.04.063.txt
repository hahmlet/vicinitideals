16.04.063 Application.

Application for a land use permit (site and design review, conditional use permit, annexation, zone change, subdivision, etc.) means a package of information that includes:

A. The application form filled out and signed by the owner;

B. Site plan and/or narrative describing the proposal;

C. List of property owners on mailing labels (1" x 2 5/8"); and

D. The application fee. (Ord. 981 section 1, 1997)
