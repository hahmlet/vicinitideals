16.04.212 Eco-roof

Eco-roof means a vegetated roof constructed for water quality and quantity control. Eco-roofs are vegetated roof covers with growing media and plants taking the place of bare membrane, gravel ballast, shingles or tiles. The number of layers and the layer placement vary from system to system and roof type, but all eco-roofs include a single to multi-ply waterproofing layer, drainage, growing media and the plants, covering at least 50% of the roof deck surface. (Ord. 1338, 2010)
