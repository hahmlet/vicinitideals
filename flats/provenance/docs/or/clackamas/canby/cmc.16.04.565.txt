16.04.565 Stealth design.

A variety of techniques used to disguise or mitigate the visual presence of telecommunications support structures, antennas, and other equipment. Including, but not limited to: screening by mature trees (75 percent or more of the pole beneath the tree canopy), mimicking common features of the urban landscape (light poles, church steeples, trees, etc.), painting antennas to match the color of supporting building walls, or roof mounting behind parapets. (Ord. 981 section 17, 1997, Ord. 1539, 2020)
