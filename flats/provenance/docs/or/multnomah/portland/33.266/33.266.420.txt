33.266.420 Transportation Impact Review in the Campus Institutional Zones
Development on a site zoned CI with a College or Medical Center use must conform to an approved Transportation Impact review.
Development that is not in conformance with an approved Transportation Impact review requires Transportation Impact review when the development:
A.                                              Increases                   the net building area on the campus by more than 20,000 square feet; or
B.                                                Increases the number of parking spaces on the campus by more than 4.
