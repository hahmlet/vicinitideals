33.100.040 Other Zoning Regulations
The regulations in this chapter state the allowed uses and the development standards for the open space zone.
Sites in overlay zones, plan districts, or with            designated historic landmarks are subject to
additional regulations.             The Official Zoning Maps indicate which sites are subject to the additional regulations.
Specific uses or development types may also be subject to regulations in the 200s series of chapters.
