33.120.200 Housing Types Allowed
A.                                  Purpose. A broad range of housing types are allowed in the multi-dwelling zones.
This range allows for efficient use of land, provides options to increase housing variety and housing opportunities, and promotes affordable and energy-efficient housing.
B.                                   Housing types.
