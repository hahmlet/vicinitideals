33.920.460 Parks And Open Areas
A.                                              Characteristics. Parks And Open Areas are uses of land focusing on natural areas, large areas consisting mostly of vegetative landscaping or outdoor recreation, community gardens, or public squares.
Lands tend to have few structures.
B.                                               Accessory uses.
Accessory uses may include club houses, maintenance facilities, concessions, caretaker's quarters, food membership distribution, and parking.
C.                                                 Examples. Examples include parks, golf courses, cemeteries, public squares, plazas, recreational trails, botanical gardens, boat launching areas, nature preserves, off-                             site mitigation, community gardens, and land used for grazing that is not part of a farm or ranch.
D.                                             Exceptions. On-site mitigation is not a Parks and Open Areas use.
