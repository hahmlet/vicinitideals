33.920.010 Purpose
This Chapter classifies land uses and activities into use categories on the basis of common functional, product, or physical characteristics.
Characteristics include the type and amount of activity, the type of customers or residents, how goods or services are sold or delivered, and certain site factors.
The use categories provide a systematic basis for assignment of present and future uses to zones.
The decision to allow or prohibit the use categories in the various zones is based on the goals and policies of the Comprehensive Plan.
