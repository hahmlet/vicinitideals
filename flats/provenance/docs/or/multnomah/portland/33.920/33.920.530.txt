33.920.530 Mining
A.                                              Characteristics. Mining includes mining or extraction of mineral or aggregate resources from the ground for off-                             site use.
B.                                               Accessory uses.
Accessory uses include storage, sorting, stockpiling, or transfer off-              site of the mined material
C.                                                 Examples. Examples include quarrying or dredging for sand, gravel or other aggregate materials; mining; and oil, gas, or geothermal drilling.
