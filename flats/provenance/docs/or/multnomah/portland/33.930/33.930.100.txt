33.930.100 Measuring Lot Widths
A.                                              Single-Dwelling zones.
In the single-dwelling zones, lot width is measured by placing a rectangle along the minimum front building setback line.
Where the setback line is curved, the rectangle is placed on the line between the intersection points of the setback line with the side lot lines.
See Figure 930-20.

The rectangle must have a minimum width equal to the minimum lot width specified for the zone in Chapter           33.610.
The rectangle must have a minimum depth of 40 feet, or extend to the rear property line, whichever is less.
The rectangle must fit entirely within the lot.
See Figure 930-20.
B.                                                  All other zones.
In all other zones, lot widths are measured from the midpoints of opposite lot lines.
See Figure 930-15.
