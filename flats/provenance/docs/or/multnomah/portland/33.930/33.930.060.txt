33.930.060 Determining Average Slope
When calculating the slope of a lot an average slope is used based on the elevations at the corners of the lot.
The average slope of a lot is calculated by subtracting the average elevation of the uphill lot line and the average elevation of the downhill lot line and dividing the sum by the average distance between the two lot lines.
The average elevation of the uphill or downhill lot line is calculated by adding the elevations at the ends of the lot line and dividing by two.
