33.930.110 Measuring Areas with Squares of Specified Dimensions
Required areas (for example, required usable outdoor areas in residential zones and the industrial zone lot standards) must be of a sufficient size and configuration so that a square measuring X by X can be placed totally within the required area.
The dimensions of the square are stated in the base zone chapters.
