33.930.055 Measuring the Area of Limited Uses
The extent of allowed                          area for a use that is limited in size is determined by adding the square footage that the use occupies including exterior eating areas.
Area devoted to mechanical equipment, elevators, and stairwells are not included.
In multi-tenant buildings common areas, such as lobbies, bathrooms and hallw ays are not included when other permitted primary uses that are not limited uses occupy the building.
Regulations in the                         base zone, overlay zone or plan district may specify additional area that is either
included or excluded from the total area, such as  parking area, exterior                       storage, or exterior display.
