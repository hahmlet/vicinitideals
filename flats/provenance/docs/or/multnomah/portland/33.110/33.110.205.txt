33.110.205 Minimum Dwelling Unit Density
A.                                                       Purpose. This standard promotes additional housing opportunities in areas of the city where services are available and restricts larger sites from being utilized for a single house.
B.                                                         When this standard applies.
The minimum dwelling unit density standard applies to new development when at least one dwelling unit is proposed.
Sites in the Constrained Sites overlay zone are exempt from this standard.
C.                                                           Minimum dwelling unit density.
1.                                                            R7. In the R7 zone, a minimum of two dwelling units are required on sites that are 14,000 square feet or larger in total site area.
2.                                                            R5. In the R5 zone, a minimum of two dwelling units are required on sites that are 10,000 square feet or larger in total site area.
3.                                                             R2.5. In the R2.5 zone, a minimum of two dwelling units are required on sites that are 5,000 square feet or larger in total site area.
