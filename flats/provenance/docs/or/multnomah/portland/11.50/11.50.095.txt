11.50.095 Appeals.

(Added by Ordinance 188816; amended by Ordinance 191988, effective January 3, 2025.)

1 A determination issued as stated in Section 11.50.090 may be appealed by the property owner of the development site or that property owner’s representative to the Code Hearings Officer, as provided for in Portland City Code Chapter 22.10.
All appeals from the Code Hearings Officer’s determination in accordance with this Section will be by writ of review as authorized by Section 22.04.010 and ORS 34.010-34.100.
