33.610.300 Through Lots
A.                                                           Purpose. This standard ensures that lots are configured in a way that development can be oriented toward streets to increase the safety and enjoyment of pedestrians and bicyclists.
The standard also ensures that development does not “turn its back” on a collector or major city traffic street.

B.                                                Standard. Through lots are allowed only where both front lot lines are on local service streets.
The minimum front lot line and minimum width standards apply to one frontage of the through lot.
