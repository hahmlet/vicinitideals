33.610.500 Split Zoned Lots
A.                                                           Purpose. This standard ensures that lots do not have more than one zone.Lots that are split by more than one zone present practical problems related to the applicability of use and development standards.

B.                                                Standard. On sites with more than one base zone, each lot must be entirely within one zone.
The creation of lots that are in more than one zone is not allowed.
