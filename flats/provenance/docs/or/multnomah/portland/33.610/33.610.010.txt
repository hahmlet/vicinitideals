33.610.010 Purpose
This chapter contains the density and lot dimension requirements for approval of a Preliminary Plan for a land division in the RF through R2.5 zones.
These requirements ensure that lots are consistent with the desired character of each zone while allowing lots to vary in size and shape provided the planned intensity of each zone is respected.
This chapter works in conjunction with other chapters of this Title to ensure that land divisions create lots that can support appropriate uses and development.
