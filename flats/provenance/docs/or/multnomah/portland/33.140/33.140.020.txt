33.140.020 List of the Employment and Industrial Zones
The full and short names of the employment and industrial zones and their map symbols are listed below.
When this Title refers to the employment or E zones it is referring to the first three listed.
When this Title refers to the industrial or I zones, it is referring to the last three listed.
