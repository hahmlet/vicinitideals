33.140.255 Drive-Through Facilities
Drive-through facilities are allowed in the zones which are intended for auto- accommodating development.
They are not consistent with or supportive of areas where the desired character is pedestrian-oriented development.
The standards for drive-through facilities are stated in Chapter 33.224, Drive-Through Facilities.

A.                                             EG and I zones.
Drive-through facilities are allowed in the EG and I zones.
B.                                                  EX zone.
Drive-through facilities are prohibited in the EX zone.
