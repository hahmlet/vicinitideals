§ 19.120.020. Permitted uses.

The following uses and their accessory uses are permitted in a VTH zone:

A.

Single-unit detached dwelling (existing only).

B.

Townhouses.

C.

Duplexes.

D.

Accessory buildings such as garages, carports, studios, private workshops, playhouses, private greenhouses or other similar structures related to the dwelling in design, whether attached or detached.

E.

Parks and playgrounds.

F.

Family day care providers and residential homes – see Chapter 19.200 FMC.

G.

Home occupations – see Chapter 19.490 FMC.

H.

Signs – see Chapter 19.170 FMC.

I.

Accessory dwelling units.

(Ord. 6-2001 § 1; Ord. 1-2024 § 1 (Att. A))
