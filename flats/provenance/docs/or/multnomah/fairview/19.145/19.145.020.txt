§ 19.145.020. Entries and front porches.

A.

Primary entries shall be accessed directly from a public street and must be visible from the street.

B.

A minimum of every other residential unit within a block frontage shall have a front porch.
Residential units without front porches shall not be constructed on adjacent lots.

C.

Front porches may encroach into front setback and are not subject to lot coverage limitations.

(Ord. 6-2001 § 1)
