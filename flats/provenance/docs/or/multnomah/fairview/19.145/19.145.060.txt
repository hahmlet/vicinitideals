§ 19.145.060. Public recreational uses.

Publicly owned and operated parks, playgrounds, recreational and community buildings and related improvements such as tennis courts, and similar recreational uses; provided, that any principal building, swimming pool, or use shall be located not less than 45 feet (VSF) or 30 feet (VTH and VA) from any other lot in the same zone.

(Ord. 6-2001 § 1)
