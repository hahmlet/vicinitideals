§ 19.145.040. Roofs.

Hipped gambrel or gabled roofs are required.
The pitch of the roof shall be no less than 4:12.
Flat roofs are not permitted except for mechanical equipment areas in VA zones.
Thirty-inch-high parapets are required along street facades where flat roofs are used.
Flat roofs are not permitted in VSF and VTH zones.

(Ord. 6-2001 § 1)
