§ 19.13.230. "W" definitions.

Wetland.
"Wetlands" are land areas where water is the dominant factor determining the nature of soil development and the types of plant and animal communities.
They are defined more specifically by the Federal Clean Water Act and Oregon Administrative Rules.

"Window hood" means an architectural detail placed above a window, used as an accent.

"Wireless communication equipment" includes cell towers, antennas, monopoles, and related facilities used for radio signal transmission and receiving (see Chapter 19.245 FMC).

(Ord. 6-2001 § 1)
