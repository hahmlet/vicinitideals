§ 19.13.110. "K" definitions.

"Kennel or other animal boarding place" means any establishment or premises where five or more dogs over one year of age are kept for any purpose whatsoever, or any establishment or premises where dogs are bred, boarded or offered for sale as a commercial business.
"Other animal boarding place" as used in this title means and includes any establishment or premises where five or more cats or other animals are bred, boarded or offered for sale as a commercial business.

(Ord. 6-2001 § 1)
