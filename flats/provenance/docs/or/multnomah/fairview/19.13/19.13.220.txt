§ 19.13.220. "V" definitions.

"Vacate plat/street" means to abandon a subdivision or street right-of-way.
For example, vacation of a public right-of-way that is not needed or cannot be used for a street or other public purpose.
A plat may be vacated, returning the property to an undivided condition.

"Variance" means an administrative or quasi-judicial decision to lessen or otherwise modify the requirements of this code.
