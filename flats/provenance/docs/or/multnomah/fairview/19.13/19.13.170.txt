§ 19.13.170. "Q" definitions.

"Quadplex" means four dwelling units on a lot or parcel in any configuration.

"Quasi-judicial" refers to an action or decision that requires substantial discretion or judgement in applying the standards or criteria of this code, and usually involves a public hearing.

(Ord. 6-2001 § 1; Ord. 1-2024 § 1 (Att. A))
