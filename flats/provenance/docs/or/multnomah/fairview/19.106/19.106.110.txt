§ 19.106.110. Emergency exemption.

The city of Fairview shall be exempt from the permit requirements of this chapter if, during a flooding event, the city manager determines that a portion of the creek channel must be immediately altered in some manner in order to avoid substantial property damage.

(Ord. 3-2012 §§ 2, 3 (Att. 2); Ord. 8-2021 § 1)
