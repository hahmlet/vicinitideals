§ 19.106.100. Floodplain ordinance.

Areas adjacent to any of the city of Fairview's water resources are also regulated by the city's floodplain overlay district.
All riparian buffer alterations must be in compliance with the applicable standards of the floodplain overlay zone before permit issuance.
Where regulations of the natural resource overlay zone differ from those found in the floodplain overlay zone, the more restrictive standards shall apply.

(Ord. 3-2012 §§ 2, 3 (Att. 2))
