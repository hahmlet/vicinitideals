§ 19.105.060. Prohibited uses.

The following uses are not permitted:

A.

Any use otherwise prohibited in the base zone or existing flood hazard overlay zone.

B.

Any uncontained area of hazardous materials as defined by DEQ.

(Formerly 19.105.070; Ord. 6-2001 § 1; Ord. 2-2019 § 2 (Exh. 1); Ord. 1-2026 § 1 (Att. A))
