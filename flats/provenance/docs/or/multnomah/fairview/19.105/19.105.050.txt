§ 19.105.050. Permitted uses.

The following uses are permitted in accordance with the specific and general standards outlined in this chapter, and following the review procedures identified in FMC 19.105.040(C)(3):

A.

All uses allowed in the base zone or existing flood hazard overlay zone.

B.

Excavation and fill required to plant any new trees or vegetation.

C.

Restoration or enhancement of floodplains, riparian areas, upland and streams.

(Formerly 19.105.060; Ord. 6-2001 § 1; Ord. 2-2019 § 2 (Exh. 1); Ord. 1-2026 § 1 (Att.
A))
