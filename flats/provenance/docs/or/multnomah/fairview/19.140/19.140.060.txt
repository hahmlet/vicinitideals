§ 19.140.060. Erosion control standards.

The site must be contoured, planted, or developed to prevent erosion, pollution, and sedimentation into adjacent natural resource areas within six weeks of the issuance of the certificate of occupancy.
Erosion control techniques must meet city of Fairview erosion control handbook standards.

(Ord. 6-2001 § 1; Ord. 11-2014 § 2 (Exh. 2))
