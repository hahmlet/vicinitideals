§ 19.165.020. Transportation standards.

Transportation standards are necessary so that the Fairview street system remains intact and well connected.
Streets are critical to the connection of neighborhoods, businesses, schools, etc.
It is important to regulate roadway sizes, locations and right-of-way dimensions.

The street standards are based directly on the Fairview transportation system plan.
Traffic calming measures are addressed in the adopted transportation system plan.
The city of Fairview has no formalized traffic-calming plan, but has recently adopted a speed hump management program.
The street alignment illustrations used in the proposed code are taken directly from the transportation system plan.

(Ord. 6-2001 § 1)
