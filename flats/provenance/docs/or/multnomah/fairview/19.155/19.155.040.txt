§ 19.155.040. Construction prior to occupancy.

The portion of the trail system located on the site for new development shall be constructed by the developer prior to occupancy.
The city of Fairview shall extend system development charge credit to the developer equal to the land costs and actual costs for trail and park development as referenced in the city parks recreation/open space master plan.

(Ord. 6-2001 § 1; Ord. 11-2014 § 3 (Exh. 3))
