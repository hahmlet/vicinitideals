§ 19.150.030. Entries.

A.

Primary entries shall face a public street or designated pedestrian way and shall be accessed from a public sidewalk.
The entries shall be open to the public during all business hours.
Secondary entries may face parking lots or loading areas.

B.

VCM upper story residential uses shall have shared or individual entries every 70 feet on the first level only; no outside staircases are allowed.

(Ord. 6-2001 § 1)
