§ 19.150.080. Screening.

A.

All primary and accessory uses associated with Village office and Village commercial uses, including storage of materials, products, or waste, shall be wholly contained within an approved structure.

B.

Parking and service areas shall be screened from pedestrian ways or public right-of-way by a three-foot-high hedge or sight-obscuring wall.

C.

Loading areas and dumpsters shall be screened from public rights-of-way and pedestrian ways by walls, trellises, fences, or landscaping.

D.

Mechanical equipment and satellite dishes over one meter in size shall be screened from view from any pedestrian way or public right-of-way.

(Ord. 6-2001 § 1)
