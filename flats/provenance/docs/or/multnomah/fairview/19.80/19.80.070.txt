§ 19.80.070. Architectural guidelines and standards.

All developments in the light industrial district shall be evaluated during site design review for conformance with the criteria in subsections A and B of this section.

A.

Building Mass. Where building elevations are oriented to the street in conformance with FMC 19.80.060, architectural features such as windows, pedestrian entrances, building off-sets, projections, detailing, change in materials or similar features, shall be used to break up and articulate large building surfaces and volumes.

B.

Pedestrian-Scale Building Entrances. Recessed entries, canopies, and/or similar features shall be used at the entries to buildings in order to create a pedestrian-scale.
