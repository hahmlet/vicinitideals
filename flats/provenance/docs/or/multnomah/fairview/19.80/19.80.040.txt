§ 19.80.040. Lot coverage.

The maximum allowable lot coverage in the light industrial district is 85 percent.
The maximum allowable lot coverage is computed by calculating the total area covered by buildings and impervious (paved) surfaces, including accessory structures, as a percentage of total lot area.
Compliance with other sections of this code may preclude development of the maximum lot coverage for some land uses.

(Ord. 6-2001 § 1)
