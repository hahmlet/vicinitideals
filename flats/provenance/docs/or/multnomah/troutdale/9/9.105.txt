9.105  Accessible Parking Facilities.

The required number of accessible parking spaces shall be in conformance with the applicable provisions of the State of Oregon Structural Specialty Code.
If all proposed parking is off-site, the off-site parking for people with disabilities must be located within the shortest possible distance to an entrance via an accessible path.
(Ord. No. 899, § 2(Att.
A), 6-24-2025)
