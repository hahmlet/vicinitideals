9.110  Off-Street Parking Restrictions.

A.     Parking spaces in a public street, including an alley, shall not be considered required parking.

B.     Required parking shall be available for parking of operable passenger vehicles of residents, customers, and employees only, and shall not be used for the storage or display of vehicles or materials.
(Ord. No. 899, § 2(Att.
A), 6-24-2025)
