3.413  GI - General Industrial.

This district is primarily intended for manufacturing industries, large-scale fabricators, freight and trucking firms, primary metals, and lumber, etc., that usually require highway access and/or rail service.
These firms usually have a high degree of process visibility and need outdoor storage of materials and products.
These industries are likely to create minor air and water pollution, as well as noise and odor, and the generation of truck, shipping, or rail traffic.
Non-industrial uses of a commercial nature are permitted in compliance with the Troutdale Development Code.
