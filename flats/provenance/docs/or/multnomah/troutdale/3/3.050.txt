3.050  National Scenic Area.

Multnomah County holds zoning authority over certain lots that are located within incorporated areas of the City of Troutdale that are within the federally-designated Columbia River Gorge National Scenic Area.
These affected lots maintain County zoning district designation in accordance with Chapters 38 and 39 of the Multnomah County Code and the official zoning district map for Multnomah County.
The City of Troutdale retains certain development standards and regulatory authority as identified in Chapter 1 of this Code and established through an effective intergovernmental agreement.
