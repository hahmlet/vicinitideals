3.410  General Purpose.

The purpose of industrial zoning districts is to provide dedicated industrial areas in certain areas of the City that are best suited for businesses that involve an industrial process and that may operate in a way that is less desirable for adjacent residential uses and certain commercial uses.
These areas are also often considered employment centers and may conduct activities at various hours of the day.
(Ord. No. 895, § 1(Att. A), 2-25-2025)
