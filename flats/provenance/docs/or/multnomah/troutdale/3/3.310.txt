3.310  General Purpose.

The purpose of commercial zoning districts is to provide dedicated commercial areas in certain areas of the City that are best suited for businesses that typically do not involve an industrial process.
The types of districts are distinctive based on the consumer catchment area and the scale and types of businesses that are most appropriate for the setting and its immediate surroundings.
(Ord. No. 895, § 1(Att.
A), 2-25-2025)
