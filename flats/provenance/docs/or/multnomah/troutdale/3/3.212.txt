3.212  MU-2 - General Mixed-Use.

This district is intended to provide a compatible mix of commercial uses and medium-density residential opportunities in certain areas across the City, including but not limited to areas adjacent to the MU-1 district.
Smaller-scale high-density residential may be appropriate in select areas.
This district is distinct from the MU-1 district in terms of having lots that are typically larger than a simple storefront, along with general expectations for maintaining on-site parking, landscaping, and screening.
This district is distinct from the MU-3 district in terms of development scale and overall impact on surrounding areas.
Specific development and design criteria for development is specified in Chapter 8 of this Code.
