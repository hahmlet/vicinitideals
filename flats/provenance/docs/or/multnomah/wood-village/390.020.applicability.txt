390.020 Applicability.

This section is included in your selections.

The mixed solid waste and source separated recyclables storage standards shall apply to new multi-unit residential buildings containing five or more units1 and non-residential construction that are subject to full site plan or design review; and are located within urban zones that allow, outright or by condition, for such uses.

1ORS 459A.010 (2)(d)
