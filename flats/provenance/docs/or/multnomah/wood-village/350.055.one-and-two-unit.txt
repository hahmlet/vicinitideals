350.055 General Regulations.

This section is included in your selections.

A. Structures These Regulations Apply To. The regulations of this section apply to houses, attached houses, duplexes, and manufactured homes. The regulations apply to required and excess parking areas. Parking for manufactured home parks is regulated in Section 340 - Manufactured Homes.

B. Parking Area Locations. Required parking spaces are not allowed within the first 10 feet from a front lot line. In addition, no more than 40 percent of the land area between the front lot line and the front building line may be paved for parking or driveway purposes. Parking in garages is subject to the base zone setback standards.

C. Parking Space Sizes. The minimum size of a required parking space is 9 feet by 19 feet. The minimum driveway width on private property is 9 feet for residential uses.

D. Paving. All driveways and parking areas shall have a durable, dust free surfacing of asphalt, concrete, or other material approved by the Director of Public Works.
