10.1744     PROCEDURES

A.                        An application shall be subject to the procedure type specified in Table 11.0204.
An application for affordable housing development is not a unique application type.
B.                          The applicant shall submit a statement with the development permit application describing how the application shall meet the applicable income restrictions, terms, and ownership standards in Section 10.1743.
The statement shall be signed by the housing and property owner(s).
C.                            Prior to building permit issuance, the applicant shall submit an affordable housing covenant recorded by the County recorder, which demonstrates that the affordability criteria in Section 10.1743(A)          are met.
