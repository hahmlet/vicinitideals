10.1732     APPLICABILITY

A.                        Land Use Districts.
This section shall apply to the following land use districts: NC, RTC, SC, CC, MC, TC-PV, NC-PV, VC-SW, and NC-SW.
B.                          Exemptions. This section does not apply to:
1.                             Property in the Floodplain Overlay District, Highly Sloped Subareas, and the Natural Resource Overlay; or
2.                             Lands that are vacant or that were added to the urban growth boundary within the last 15 years.
C.                            Overlapping Criteria.
For a proposed affordable housing development that meets the criteria in this section as well as the criteria in Section 10.1700 Affordable Housing with Height and Density Bonuses or Section 10.1740 Affordable Housing Developed by Religious Corporation, the applicant may choose which set of standards to follow.
