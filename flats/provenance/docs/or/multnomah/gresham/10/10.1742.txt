10.1742     APPLICABILITY

A.                        Land Use Districts.
This section shall apply on land that is in any land use district except for the following industrial districts: HI, GI, ME-PV, RTI-SW, and IND-SW.
B.                          Overlapping Criteria.
For a proposed affordable housing development that meets the criteria in this section as well as the criteria in Section 10.1700 Affordable Housing with Height and Density Bonuses or Section 10.1730 Affordable Housing on Commercial Land, the applicant may choose which set of standards to follow.
