4.1117                                                                  SPLIT-ZONED DEVELOPMENT SITES When a single development site in the Downtown Plan sub-district is affected by two or more of the following sub  - districts, the entire site may be developed in conformance with permitted uses and development standards of any of those sub-districts applying to the site: DCC, DMU, DTM, DEM, DRL-1, DRL-2, and DCL.
