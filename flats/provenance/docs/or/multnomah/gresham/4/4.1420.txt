4. 1420  TOWN  CENTER – PLEASANT VALLEY (TC -PV)
The town center is intended to be the heart of the Pleasant Valley community.
It will contain a mix of retail, office, civic, and housing uses in a pedestrian-oriented environment.
The town center shall accommodate the daily needs of the local community.
It shall be served by a multi-modal transportation system with strong vehicular, pedestrian, bicycle, and transit access.
A. Characteristics. The Town Center (TC-PV) sub-district has the following characteristics:
1. The Town Center sub-district permits a wide range of housing types, including live-work uses, mixed-use buildings, and adjacent townhouses and apartments.
2. Streets and buildings are designed to emphasize a lively, pedestrian-oriented character where people feel safe day and night.
3. A central green(s) or plaza(s) provides a community gathering space(s) on large development sites.
4. The town center has strong connections to adjacent neighborhoods.
5. The town center is anchored (at least) by a grocery store.
Smaller buildings for retail and service uses, civic uses, and mixed commercial/residential uses shall also be oriented on pedestrian main streets(s) and plaza(s).
6. The town center is an easy and attractive place to walk, bike, drive, and use transit.
