4.0150                             POULTRY, LIVESTOCK, AND BEEKEEPING

The keeping of poultry and livestock accessory to a residential use is permitted in the LDR-5, TR, TLDR, MDR- 12, and MDR-24 districts provided the poultry is kept 25 feet or more from any residence and livestock are kept over 100 feet from any residence,                                      except the residence of the owner of the poultry and livestock.
See the Gresham Revised Code for additional requirements related to the keeping of chickens and for standards related to beekeeping.
