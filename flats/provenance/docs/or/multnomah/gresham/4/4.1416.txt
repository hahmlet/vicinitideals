4.14 16  BUILDING HEIGHT AND HEIGHT TRANSITION STANDARDS
A. Applicability. The following standards apply to buildings to be built on lots in the HDR-PV, TC-PV, NCPV, and ME-PV sub-districts when they abut the LDR-PV land use district.
B. At the minimum building setback from a lot line abutting a lot in the LDR-PV land use district, the maximum building height shall be 35 feet.
For every one foot of additional building setback beyond 35 feet, the maximum building height shall increase by one foot, up to the maximum total building height for the sub-district as illustrated in Figure 4.1416.
