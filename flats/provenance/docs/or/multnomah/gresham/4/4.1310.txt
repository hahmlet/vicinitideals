4.1310                              ALLOWED USES

The permitted uses of Table 4.0120             under LDR-5 apply.
The LDR-5 development types are allowed except the Gresham Butte Plan District specifically prohibits:
1.                             Triplexes;
2.                             Quadplexes;
3.                             Townhouse;
4.                              Cottage Cluster; and
5.                              Zero Lot Line Dwellings.
