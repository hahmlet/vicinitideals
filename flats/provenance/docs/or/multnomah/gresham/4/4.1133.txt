4.1133                                      LIMITATIONS ON DRIVE-THROUGHS
A.                        New drive-through facilities are limited in DCC, DMU and DEM and allowed only under the following conditions:
1.                             In DCC, no new drive-through facility can be constructed unless there was a legal drive-through on the site on July 16, 2009.
2.                             In all sub-districts where drive-throughs are limited:
a.                             Direct drive-through lane access onto an Urban Boulevard street type is prohibited.
b.                           The drive-through shall be part of a development that meets the minimum FAR requirements of the sub  - district.
c.                                 Drive-through stacking lanes and service areas shall not be located between the street and the building and shall be located to the rear of the property.
d.                           Goods and services provided to drive-through customers also must be available to pedestrian customers inside a building on the same site.
B.                          Drive-through uses are not permitted in the DRL  -1, DRL-                     2, and DTM districts.
C.                            Drive-through uses are permitted in the DCL district.
