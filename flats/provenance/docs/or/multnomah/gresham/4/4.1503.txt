4.1503  SPRINGWATER SUB -DISTRICTS IN GENERAL
The Plan District Sub-districts listed below apply to land in the Plan District.
They are intended to work together to result in a complete community that includes attractive places to live, work, shop, and recreate, together with natural resource areas that are integrated into the urban environment, consistent with the purposes in Section 4.1501 and the Comprehensive Plan.
The Sub-districts in Springwater are:

• Very Low-Density Residential - Springwater (VLDR - SW)
• Low-Density Residential - Springwater (LDR - SW)
• Townhouse Residential - Springwater (THR - SW)
