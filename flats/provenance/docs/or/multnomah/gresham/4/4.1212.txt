4.1212                                      CIVIC NEIGHBORHOOD TRANSIT MODERATE DENSITY (CNTM) The CNTM sub-district includes convenient access to arterial streets as well as local and regional transit routes including light rail stations, bus lines, and bike and pedestrian paths.
This sub  -district is intended to evolve over time from a shopping center largely organized around automobile trips and parking, into a full-service area with a diverse mix of jobs, housing, and commercial opportunities.
The sub-district allows for the widest range of intensities in the neighborhood, supporting diverse projects with varied uses, scales, and ownership structures.
