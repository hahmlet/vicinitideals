4.0412                             CORRIDOR MULTI    -   FAMILY (CMF)

This district designation is applied to properties along segments of Transit Streets.
The CMF district primarily provides opportunities for moderate-density residential development, including multi-          family and middle housing.
It also allows single detached dwellings along the NE Glisan Street and NE 162nd Avenue corridors.
To help create a pleasant pedestrian environment, most new residential buildings will be oriented to public sidewalks, with parking lots behind or beside buildings.
Standards will require that the scale of new housing diminish as buildings approach abutting lower density residential districts.
