4.1433 SETBACKS
Required minimum setbacks are as specified below.
A. Building setbacks.
Buildings must be set back from all property lines a minimum of 20 feet.
B. Outdoor activity facility setbacks.
Outdoor activity facilities, such as swimming pools, basketball courts, tennis courts, or baseball diamonds must be set back at least 50 feet from abutting residentially zoned properties.
Playground facilities must be set back at least 20 feet from abutting residentially zoned properties.
Where the outdoor activity facility abuts a residential property occupied by a school use, the required setback is reduced to zero.
