b.                           For pitched, hipped, or gambrel roofs, the solar energy system panels shall not exceed 18 inches in height from the surface of the roof on which the system is installed.
2.                             Ground-mounted. Ground-mounted solar energy systems shall not exceed 20 feet in height.
D.                       Setbacks and Yards.
Solar energy systems are not allowed in the required front or street - side setback.
