4.141 7 NEIGHBORHOOD AMENITIES
A. Purpose. The purpose of this section is to ensure that residential development in Pleasant Valley provides adequate open space consistent with the Plan District.
B. Applicability. Except as provided in Standard 4.1417(B)(3), the requirements of Section 7.0451 – Neighborhood Amenities apply if Standard 4.1417(B)(1) and Standard 4.1417(B)(2) are met.
Deviations from the standards in this section shall be subject to review through a Planned Development application (Section 6.0300).
1. The development site includes at least 2.5 acres within the LDR-PV, MDR-PV, and/or HDR-PV sub-district; and
2. The proposed development includes single-detached dwellings, duplexes, triplexes, quadplexes, or townhouses.

3. Section 4.1417 does not apply to the development of multi-family uses, manufactured dwelling parks, or cottage clusters (or the portion of a site devoted to those uses).
The open space provisions in
Section 7.0100 apply to multi-family uses.
The interior open space design standards in Section 7.0410(C) apply to manufactured dwelling parks.
The common courtyard design standards in Section 7.0400(C) apply to cottage clusters.
