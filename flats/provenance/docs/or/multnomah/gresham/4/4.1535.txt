4.1535  BUFFERING AND SCREENING
A. The standards of Section 9.0100 - Buffering and Screening - apply.
B. Buffering in the IND-SW sub-district is not required when adjacent parcels are outside City limits.
C. Buffers and screens shall have on-going maintenance.
D. Buffer and screening landscaping shall comply with the landscaping requirements of Section 4.1566 – Xeriscape Landscaping Requirements.
