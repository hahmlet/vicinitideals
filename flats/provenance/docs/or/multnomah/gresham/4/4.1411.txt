4.1411  MEDIUM -DENSITY RESIDENTIAL – PLEASANT VALLEY (MDR- PV)
The Medium-Density Residential (MDR-PV) sub-district provides a range of detached and attached dwelling units.
Development in this sub-district shall be arranged to form part of an individual neighborhood, as well as serve as a transition between the low density residential sub-district and the employment or high-density subdistricts.
A mix of housing types, forms, and designs in the MDR-PV sub-district and in individual neighborhoods is intended.
