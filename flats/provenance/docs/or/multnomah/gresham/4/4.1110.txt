4.1110                                       DOWNTOWN COMMERCIAL CORE (DCC)
The DCC is the City ’s long-standing center and features unique local businesses, small- scale storefronts, and intimate sidewalks.
Main Avenue has a small-                                            scale, walkable quality appreciated by residents and visitors.
This sub-district is
intended to preserve this small-                                              scale character on Main Avenue while encouraging an active, engaging mix of old and new uses.
The DCC sub-district allows a wide range of uses – retail, service, office and residential – that will help create a vibrant sub-district that is active all day and much of the night.
