4.1214                                       SPLIT-ZONED DEVELOPMENT SITES
When a single development site is affected by both the CNTM and the CNTH sub   -districts, those areas of the site may be
developed in conformance with permitted uses and development standards of either the CNTM or the CNTH sub    -district.
If a split-zone property includes a portion in the CNRM district, that portion shall be developed according to the CNRM standards.
