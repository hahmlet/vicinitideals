4.1112                                        DOWNTOWN TRANSIT MID-RISE (DTM)
This mixed-use sub-district provides a mid-rise, mixed-use character in the center of Downtown near the light-rail stations.
Because of its proximity to transit, this sub  -district provides access opportunities for those who live Downtown to use buses and MAX light rail to get to jobs and other destinations.
It also supports the creation of employment uses Downtown so those who live outside the Downtown have opportunities and easy access to work Downtown.
This sub-district supports the continued presence of institutional uses, such as government offices.
It also allows a mix of residential, commercial,                                             and employment uses at a mid-rise intensity.
