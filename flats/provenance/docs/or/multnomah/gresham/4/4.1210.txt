4.1210                                       CIVIC NEIGHBORHOOD SUB-DISTRICTS
The Civic Neighborhood is divided into the following three sub-districts:
•                                            Civic Neighborhood Residential Mid-Rise (CNRM)
•                                            Civic Neighborhood Transit Moderate Density (CNTM)
•                                            Civic Neighborhood Transit High Density (CNTH) Development within each sub-district is governed by a different combination of basic regulations such as uses, height limits, allowable floor area ratios, and densities.
