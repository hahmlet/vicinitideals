4.1504  VERY LOW -DENSITY RESIDENTIAL – SPRINGWATER (VLDR -SW)
The Very Low-Density Residential District is primarily intended for Single Detached Dwellings and Middle Housing at an average lot size of 12,000 square feet, and at a maximum net density for Single Detached Dwellings of 3.6 units per acre, and for Townhouses of 14.4 units per acre.
There is no minimum density in this zone.
