4.1115                                                                  DOW NTOW N RESIDENTIAL LOW-RISE-2 (DRL-2)
This mixed-use sub-district will allow a gradual transformation into more varied and full-                     service residential neighborhoods that can take advantage of their proximity to transit and nearby shopping and job centers.
This predominantly residential sub-district will allow single detached dwellings to remain while also allowing duplexes, triplexes, quadplexes, townhouses, cottage clusters, small- scale multi-family, and small-                                             scale commercial activities.
