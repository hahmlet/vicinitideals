4.1430 PUBLIC LAND – PLEASANT VALLEY (PL- PV)
The Public Land sub-district of Pleasant Valley is intended to provide public awareness of the possible uses of public land, to accommodate community-serving uses, and to provide minimum standards for the development of parks and schools.
The PL-PV sub-district includes land that serves the cultural, educational, recreational, and public service needs of Pleasant Valley and the larger community.
PL-PV is reserved for designated public facilities and shall only apply to land owned by governmental agencies for public use or benefit.
Other publicly owned lands not included within the PL-PV sub-district shall be subject to the development standards of the sub-district in which they are located.
