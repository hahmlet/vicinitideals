4.1132                                       LIMITATIONS ON OUTDOOR COMMERCIAL ACTIVITY In all Downtown sub-districts except the DCL sub-district, the amount of site area for outdoor display of materials for retail sales shall not exceed 15 percent of the ground-floor area of buildings on the site with which the display is associated or 1,000 square feet, whichever is less.
Areas devoted to on-site outdoor business activities, product display, or storage shall be located so that they do not interfere with pedestrian circulation.
