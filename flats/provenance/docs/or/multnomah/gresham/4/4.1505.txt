4.1505  LOW -DENSITY RESIDENTIAL – SPRINGWATER (LDR- SW)
The Low-Density Residential District consists of primarily Single Detached Dwellings and Middle Housing at an average net density for single detached units of 5.8 to 7.3 dwelling units per acre and 5.8 to 25 units per acre for Townhouses.
It is intended to provide for standard lot (6,000-7,500 sq. ft.) housing developments.
