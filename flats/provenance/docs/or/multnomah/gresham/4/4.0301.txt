4.0301                                       PURPOSE

This section of the Community Development Code implements  Section 10.313 -                          Industrial Land Use within
Volume 2 of the Comprehensive Plan.
The purposes of the Industrial Land Use Districts are to: (1) implement the Comprehensive Plan’s goals, policies, and action measures to promote economic development (2) include provisions to support viable industrial trends; and, (3) provide clear and objective standards so that implementation of the Industrial Land Use Districts is efficient, effective, and fair.
