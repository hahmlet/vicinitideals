4.1114                                                                  DOW NTOW N RESIDENTIAL LOW-RISE-1 (DRL-1) This mixed-use sub-district will encourage some residential areas to gently transform into a broader mix of residential uses.
This sub-district is intended to create distinctive, walkable neighborhoods within a short distance of transit and the Downtown core .
The sub-district encourages single detached dwellings to remain and allows duplexes, triplexes, quadplexes, townhouses, and cottage clusters, but not multi -family.
This sub-district also will allow small-                                            scale commercial uses only on certain streets where it is most appropriate.
