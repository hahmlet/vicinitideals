9.0610                              HEIGHT TRANSITION STANDARDS

A.                        On the applicable lots (see Figure 9.0610(A) ):
1.                             On the portion of the site less than or equal to 35 feet from the lot line of a lot zoned LDR   -5, LDR7, TR or TLDR, the maximum building height shall be 30 feet.
2.                             On the portion of the site more than 35 feet but less than or equal to 50 feet from the lot line of a lot zoned LDR-5, LDR-7, TR or TLDR, the maximum building height shall be 45 feet.
