9    .0201                             STREET AND RAILROAD CLEAR VISION AREA

A clear-                                       vision area shall be maintained on each corner of property at the intersection of two streets or a street and a railroad.
The clear vision area is triangular and extends to the curb of the intersecting street, restricting sight obstructions in the right of way as well as on a private property.
The dimensions of the clear vision area and exceptions area described in the Public Works Standards (6.04).
