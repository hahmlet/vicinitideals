§ 39.4240 SINGLE FAMILY DWELLINGS
CONDITION OF APPROVAL -
PROHIBITION ON CLAIMS
ALLEGING INJURY FROM
FARM OR FOREST
PRACTICES.
As a condition of approval of a single family dwelling, the landowner for the dwelling shall sign and record in the deed records for the county a document binding the landowner, and the landowner's successors in interest, prohibiting them from pursuing a claim for relief or cause of action alleging injury from farming or forest practices for which no action or claim is allowed under ORS 30.936 or 30.937.
