§ 39.4215 USES.
No building, structure or land shall be used and no building or structure shall be hereafter erected, altered or enlarged in this base zone except for the uses listed in MCC 39.4220 through 39.4230 when found to comply with
MCC 39.4245 through 39.4260 provided such uses occur on a Lot of Record, except those uses listed in ORS 215.283(1) may occur on
Exclusive Farm Use zone lands regardless of Lot of Record status of the land upon which the use will occur.
(Ord. 1318, Amended, 11/30/2023)
