§ 39.4785 LOT SIZES FOR
CONDITIONAL USES.
Except as otherwise established by this Chapter, the lot size for a conditional use shall be determined by the approval authority at the time of approval if the use, based upon:
(A) The site size needs of the proposed use;
(B) The nature of the proposed use in relation to its impacts on nearby properties; and
(C) Consideration of the purposes of the base zone.
