§ 39.8015   EXCEPTIONS.
The provisions of MCC 39.8000 through
39.8050 shall not be applied to the following:
(A) Single family residences.
(B) Type C Home Occupations unless located in the RC, BRC, OR, PH-RC or SRC base zones.
(C) Type C Home Occupations located in the RC, BRC, OR, PH-RC, or SRC base zones that require the addition of less than 400 square feet of ground coverage.
(D) Commercial photovoltaic solar power generation facility.
