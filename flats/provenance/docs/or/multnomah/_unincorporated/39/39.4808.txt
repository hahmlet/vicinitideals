§ 39.4808 SOLAR ACCESS PERMIT
PROVISIONS.
The purpose of MCC 39.4809 through 39.4812 is to protect solar access features on lots designated or used for a single family detached dwelling under some circumstances.
It authorizes owners of such lots to apply for a permit that, if granted, prohibits solar features from being shaded by certain future vegetation on and off the permittee’s site.
