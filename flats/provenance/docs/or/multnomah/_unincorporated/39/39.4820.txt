§ 39.4820 USES.
Except as otherwise provided in this Chapter, no building, structure or land shall be used and no building or structure shall be hereafter erected, altered or enlarged in this base zone except for the uses listed in MCC 39.4822 through 39.4826 provided such uses occur on a Lot of Record.
(Ord. 1309, Amended, 08/18/2022)
