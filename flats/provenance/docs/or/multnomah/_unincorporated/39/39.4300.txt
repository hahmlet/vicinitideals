39.4300- PURPOSE.
The purposes of the Multiple Use Agriculture base zone are to conserve those agricultural lands not suited to full-time commercial farming for diversified or part-time agriculture uses; to encourage the use of non-agricultural lands for other purposes, such as forestry, outdoor recreation, open space, low density residential development and appropriate Conditional Uses, when these uses are shown to be compatible with the agricultural uses, natural resource base, the character of the area and the applicable
County policies.
