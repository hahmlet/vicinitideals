§ 39.4860 TWO-UNIT DWELLING
LOCATIONAL
REQUIREMENTS.
A two-unit dwelling structure, approved under
MCC 39.4856(C) may be located only on a lot having access from a public street created under
MCC 39.9000 through 39.9700, when not more than four such structures having access from the same public street are located within 200 feet of each other.
