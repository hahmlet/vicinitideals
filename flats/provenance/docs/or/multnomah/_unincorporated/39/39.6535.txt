§ 39.6535  CHANGE OF USE.
(A) Any alteration of the use of any land or structure under which an increase in the number of parking or loading spaces is required by this
Subpart shall be unlawful unless the additional spaces are provided.
(B) In case of enlargement or change of use, the number of parking or loading spaces required shall be based on the total area involved in the enlargement or change in use.
