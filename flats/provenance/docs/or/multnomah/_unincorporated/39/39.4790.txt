§ 39.4790 APPLICABILITY.
The solar design standard in Section .6815 shall apply to applications for a development to create lots in LR-10, LR-7, and LR-5 zones and for single family detached dwellings, except to the extent the approval authority finds that the applicant has shown one or more of the conditions listed in MCC 39.4792 and MCC
39.4793 exist, and exemptions or adjustments provided for therein are warranted.
