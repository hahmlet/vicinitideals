§ 39.6510   CONTINUING OBLIGATION.
The provision for and maintenance of off-street parking and loading facilities without charge to users shall be a continuing obligation of the property owner.
No building or any other required permit for a structure or use under this or any other applicable rule, ordinance or regulation shall be issued until satisfactory evidence in the form of a site development plan, plans of existing parking and loading improvements, a deed, lease, contract or similar document is presented demonstrating that the property is and will remain available for the designated use as a parking or loading facility.
