38.3000- PURPOSES

The purposes of the Gorge General Residential and
Gorge Special Residential districts are to ensure that residential development outside urban areas does not adversely affect the scenic, cultural, natur al and recreation resources of the Columbia River Gorge
National Scenic Area  and to ensure that residential development outside of urban areas protects agricultural and forest land for agricultural and forest uses.
(Ord. 1302, Amended, 12/16/2021; Ord. 997, Repealed and Replaced,
10/31/2002; Ord. 953 §2, Reorg&Renum, 11/30/2000)
