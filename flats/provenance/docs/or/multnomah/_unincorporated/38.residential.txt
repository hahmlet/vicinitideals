38.3000- PURPOSES

The purposes of the Gorge General Residential and
Gorge Special Residential districts are to ensure that
residential development outside urban areas does
not adversely affect the scenic, cultural, natur al and
recreation resources of the Columbia River Gorge
National Scenic Area  and to ensure that residential
development outside of urban areas protects agricul-
tural and forest land for agricultural and forest uses.
