16.106.070 Bike Lanes
If shown in Figure 13 of the Transportation System Plan, bicycle lanes shall be installed in public rights-of-way, in accordance with City specifications.
Bike lanes shall be installed on both sides of designated roads, should be separated from the road by a twelve-inch stripe or other means approved by Engineering Staff, and should be a minimum of five (5) feet wide.
(Ord. No. 2014-012, § 3, 7-17-2014; Ord. No. 2011-011, § I, 10-4-2011)
