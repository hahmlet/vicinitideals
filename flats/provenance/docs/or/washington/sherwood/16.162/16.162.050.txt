16.162.050 Prohibited Uses
The following uses are expressly prohibited in the OT overlay zone, notwithstanding whether such uses are permitted outright or conditionally in the underlying RC, HDR or MDRL zones:
A. Adult entertainment businesses.
B. Manufactured homes on individual lots.
C. Manufactured home parks.
D. Restaurants with drive-through.
(Ord. 2002-1128 § 3; 94-990; 92-946; 87-859)
E. Stand alone cellular or wireless communication towers and facilities.
Co-location of existing legally permitted facilities is acceptable.
(Ord. 2006-009 § 2)
