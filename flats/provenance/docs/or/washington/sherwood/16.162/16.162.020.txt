16.162.020 Objectives
Land use applications within the Old Town Overlay District must demonstrate substantial conformance with the standards and criteria below:
A. Encourage development that is compatible with the existing natural and man-made environment, existing community activity patterns, and community identity.
B. Minimize or eliminate adverse visual, aesthetic or environmental effects caused by the design and location of new development, including but not limited to effects from:
1. The scale, mass, height, areas, appearances and architectural design of buildings and other development structures and features.
2. Vehicular and pedestrian ways and parking areas.

3. Existing or proposed alteration of natural topographic features, vegetation and waterways.
(Ord. 2002-1128 § 3; 94-990)
