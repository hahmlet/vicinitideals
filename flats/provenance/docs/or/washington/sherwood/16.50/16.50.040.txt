16.50.040 Accessory Structure Exemptions
The following are not considered accessory structures, for the purposes of this section:
A. Pergolas, arbors and trellises and other similar structures, if under ten (10) feet.
B. Play structure and swing sets if under ten (10) feet.
C. Flag poles limited to 20 feet
D. Temporary and seasonal above ground pools
E. Structures that are considered Accessory Dwelling Units and fall under the provisions of 16.52 Accessory Dwelling Units.
(Ord. No. 2011-003, § 2, 4-5-2011)
