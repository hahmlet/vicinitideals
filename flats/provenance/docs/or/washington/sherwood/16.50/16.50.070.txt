16.50.070 In Ground Pools
A. In-ground pools/spas less than 3 feet in height that are not temporary or seasonal may be sited 5 feet from the side and 10 feet from the rear property lines.
In-ground pools shall not be placed within the required front or street side setback.
(Ord. No. 2018-007, § 2, 10-2-2018)
