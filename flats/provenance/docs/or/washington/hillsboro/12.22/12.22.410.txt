12.22.410  Purposes.
A. Provide Housing Options in Proximity to Transit.
The SCR-MD zone implements the SCPA Station Community Planning Area designation, providing an opportunity for medium density residential development generally within 1,300 to 2,600 feet of a light rail station.
B. Transition Residential Density.
The SCR-MD zone may also be applied to serve as a transition area between lower-density neighborhoods and higher intensity development nearer light rail stations.
C. Preserve Neighborhood Livability.
The SCR-MD zone encourages livability in residential neighborhoods by allowing residential development with compatible non-residential Uses such as schools, assembly facilities, parks, and appropriately-scaled neighborhood commercial Uses.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022; Ord. 6393 § 1, 2021)
