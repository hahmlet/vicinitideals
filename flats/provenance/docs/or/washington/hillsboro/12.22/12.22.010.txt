12.22.010  Multi-Dwelling Residential Zones.
There are 5 multi-dwelling residential zones in the City:
A. MR-1 Multi-Dwelling Residential;
B. MR-2 Multi-Dwelling Residential;
C. MR-3 Multi-Dwelling Residential;
D. SCR-MD Station Community Residential—Medium Density; and
E. SCR-HD Station Community Residential—High Density.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022)
