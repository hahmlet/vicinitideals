12.22.310  Purposes.
A. Create and Maintain Middle Housing and Multi-Dwelling Residential Opportunities.
The MR-3 zone implements the Mid-Rise Residential Comprehensive Plan designation, providing an opportunity for higher density residential development in appropriate areas.
B. Preserve Neighborhood Livability.
The MR-3 zone encourages livability in existing and future residential neighborhoods by allowing residential development with compatible non-residential Uses such as schools, assembly facilities and parks.
