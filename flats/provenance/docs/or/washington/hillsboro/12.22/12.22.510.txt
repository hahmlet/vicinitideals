12.22.510  Purposes.
A. Provide Housing Options in Proximity to Transit.
The SCR-HD zone implements the SCPA Station Community Planning Area designation, providing an opportunity for higher density residential development generally within 1,300 feet of a light rail station.
B. Preserve Neighborhood Livability.
The SCR-HD zone encourages livability in high density residential neighborhoods by allowing residential development with compatible non-residential Uses such as schools, assembly facilities, parks, and with appropriately-scaled neighborhood commercial Uses.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022; Ord. 6393 § 1, 2021)
