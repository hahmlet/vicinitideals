12.40.020  Applicability.
Specific uses listed in this subchapter are subject to the base zone standards in Subchapters 12.21 through 12.26, the development and design standards in Subchapter 12.50, and any applicable overlay zone standards or plan district standards in Subchapters 12.27 or 12.60 through 12.66.
Where special use standards conflict with other standards in this Code, refer to Section 12.01.430 for the hierarchy of standards.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022; Ord. 6322 § 1, 2019)
