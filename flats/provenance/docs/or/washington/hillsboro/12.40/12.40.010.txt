12.40.010  Purpose.
This subchapter lists development requirements for specific uses which may be permitted or permitted as conditional uses in one or more zones.
Specific standards are necessary for these uses due to their potential impacts on neighboring properties.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022; Ord. 6322 § 1, 2019; Ord. 6294 § 1, 2019)
