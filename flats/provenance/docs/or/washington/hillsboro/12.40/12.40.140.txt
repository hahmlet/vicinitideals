12.40.140  Cemeteries.
A. Characteristics. Cemeteries are land used or intended to be used for interment of the dead.
Interment structures such as mausoleums and columbariums, and structures for associated Uses such as mortuaries and crematoria may be included within the boundaries of a cemetery.
Accessory structures may include chapels, maintenance buildings and caretakers' dwellings.
B. Approval Process.
Where allowed, cemeteries shall be subject to review and approval under
Sections 12.80.020 and 12.80.040.
C. Standards. Applications for new cemeteries, mausoleums, or columbariums shall comply with the following standards:
1. Minimum Lot Size. The minimum lot size shall be 2 acres if used for earthen burials.
The minimum lot size for mausoleums and columbariums without earthen burials shall be that of the applicable base zone.
2. Minimum Lot Size with Accessory Uses. The minimum lot size shall be 5 acres where a mortuary, crematorium, columbarium or mausoleum is included in the cemetery.
3. Fencing, Landscaping and Irrigation.
Fencing, landscaping and irrigation shall be provided consistent with Sections 12.50.220 and 12.50.250.
4. Plat Requirements. A plat and maintenance agreement shall be recorded prior to sale of lots.
5. State Requirements. Cemetery operation shall continuously conform to applicable State standards.
