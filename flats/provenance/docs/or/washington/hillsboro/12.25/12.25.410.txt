12.25.410  Purpose.
A. Provide a Range of Industrial Activities.
The SCBP zone implements the Station Community Planning Area Plan designation, providing opportunities for light industrial and industrial service uses, and limited opportunities for convenient service and office commercial uses, up to 5,200 feet from an LRT Station.
B. Create High-Quality, Attractive Industrial Parks.
The standards of the SCBP zone are intended to create high-quality industrial neighborhoods incorporating development and design elements to create transit-supportive and pedestrian-friendly yet automobile-accommodating developments.
C. Reduce Vehicle Miles Traveled.
The SCBP zone allows light industrial uses and limited commercial uses in light rail zones, thereby reducing vehicle miles travelled by industrial employees for commuting and convenience trips.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022)
