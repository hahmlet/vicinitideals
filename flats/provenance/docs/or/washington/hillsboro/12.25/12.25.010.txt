12.25.010  Industrial Zones.
There are 8 industrial zones in the City:
A. I-G Industrial General;
B. I-P Industrial Park;
C. I-S Industrial Sanctuary;
D. SCBP Station Community Business Park;
E. SCI Station Community Industrial;
F. SSID Shute Road Special Industrial District;
G. ESID Evergreen Area Special Industrial District; and
H. HSID Helvetia Area Special Industrial District.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022)
