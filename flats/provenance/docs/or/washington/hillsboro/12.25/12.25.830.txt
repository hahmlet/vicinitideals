12.25.830  Uses.
Table 12.25.830-1 lists uses permitted, conditionally permitted, limited, or not permitted in the HSID zone.
Unless a use is permitted or conditionally permitted in Table 12.25.830-1 or is determined to be permissible through a Director's Interpretation under Section 12.80.050, the use shall be excluded from, and may not be permitted to develop within the HSID Zone.
