12.25.170  Variances and Adjustments.
Applications for Variances and Adjustments to base zone standards in the I-G zone may be approved under 1 of 4 processes:
A. As a Variance pursuant to Section 12.80.152;
B. As a Minor Adjustment in conjunction with a primary Type II or Type III application pursuant to
Section 12.80.154;
C. As a Major Adjustment in conjunction with a primary Type III application pursuant to Section 12.80.156; or
D. With a Significant Natural Resource Permit pursuant to Section 12.80.130.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022; Ord. 6120 § 1, 2015)
