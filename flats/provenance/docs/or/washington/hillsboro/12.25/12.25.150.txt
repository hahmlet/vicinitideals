12.25.150  Special Provisions Regarding Uses.
A. Retail Products and Services.
1. Day care facilities are permitted with Conditional Use approval, subject to the additional standards in Section 12.40.150.
2. Outdoor exercise areas for animal service facilities are permitted with Conditional Use approval, and are subject to the additional standards in Section 12.40.110.
3. Minor assembly facilities are permitted with Conditional Use approval, and are subject to additional regulations in Section 12.40.210.
B. Aviation Uses.
The following types of aviation uses are limited to properties owned by the Port of Portland at the Portland-Hillsboro Airport: runways; taxiways; aprons; air operations areas for unobstructed movement of aircraft; security areas; terminals and associated parking; and aircraft storage hangars.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022; Ord. 6294 § 1, 2019; Ord. 6149 § 1, 2015)
