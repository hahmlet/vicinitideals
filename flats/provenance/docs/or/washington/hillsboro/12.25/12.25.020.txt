12.25.020  Locational Characteristics.
On the Comprehensive Plan Land Use Map, industrial zones implement the Industrial and Station Community Planning Area designations.
A. The I-G, I-P, I-S, SSID, ESID and HSID zones implement or partially implement both the Industrial and the Employment designations on the Metro Functional Plan Map.
B. Within the SCPA designation, the SCBP and SCI zones allow development of higher intensity, transit-supportive industrial Uses in proximity to light rail stations.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022)
