12.21.010  Single Detached and Middle Housing Residential Zones.
There are 8 single detached and middle housing residential zones in the City:
A. R-10 Residential;
B. R-8.5 Residential;
C. R-7 Residential;
D. R-6 Residential;
E. R-4.5 Residential;
F. SCR-LD Station Community Residential—Low Density;
G. SCR-OTC Station Community Residential—Orenco Townsite Conservation; and
H. SCR-DNC Station Community Residential—Downtown Neighborhood Conservation.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022)
