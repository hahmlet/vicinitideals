12.21.110  Purposes.
A. Create and Maintain Large Lot Residential Opportunities.
The R-10 zone implements the lowest density of the Low Density Residential Comprehensive Plan designation, providing an opportunity for large lot residential development in appropriate areas.
B. Preserve Neighborhood Livability.
The R-10 zone encourages livability in existing and future large lot residential neighborhoods by allowing residential development with compatible non-residential uses such as schools, assembly facilities and parks.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022)
