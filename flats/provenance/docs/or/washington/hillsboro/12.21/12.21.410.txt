12.21.410  Purposes.
A. Create and Maintain Smaller Lot Residential Opportunities.
The R-6 zone implements the highest end of the Low Density Residential Comprehensive Plan designation density range, providing an opportunity for smaller lot residential development in appropriate areas.
B. Preserve Neighborhood Livability.
The R-6 zone encourages livability in existing and future smaller lot residential neighborhoods by allowing residential development with compatible non-residential Uses such as schools, assembly facilities and parks.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022)
