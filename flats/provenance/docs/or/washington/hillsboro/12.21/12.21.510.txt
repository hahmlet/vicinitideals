12.21.510  Purposes.
A. Create and Maintain Small Lot Residential Opportunities.
The R-4.5 zone implements the lowest density of the Medium Density Residential Comprehensive Plan designation, providing an opportunity for small lot residential development in appropriate areas.
B. Preserve Neighborhood Livability.
The R-4.5 zone encourages livability in existing and future small lot residential neighborhoods by allowing residential development with compatible non-residential Uses such as schools, assembly facilities and parks.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022)
