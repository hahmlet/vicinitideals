12.66.360  Vehicle Parking and Loading: Design and Improvements.
In addition to the standards of Section 12.50.360, within the North Hillsboro Industrial Area Plan District the following standards shall apply.
A. Structured Parking.
1. Structured parking within or adjacent to a primary structure shall be reflective of the overall design of the primary structure it serves through the use of visually similar architectural features and façade materials.
2. Free-standing parking structures shall be designed to complement surrounding buildings in terms of scale, design elements, building materials, and orientation on the site.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022; Ord. 6274 § 1, 2018)
