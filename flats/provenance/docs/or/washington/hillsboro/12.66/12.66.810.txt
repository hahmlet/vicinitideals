12.66.810  Purpose and Applicability.
A. Purpose. These standards are intended to ensure that industrial and commercial developments are designed to be consistent with the policies in the North Hillsboro Industrial Area Community Plan.
B. Applicability. Within the North Hillsboro Industrial Area Plan District, the design standards for non-residential and mixed-use development in Sections 12.50.800 through 12.50.890 shall apply except as modified or superseded by the standards in this section.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022; Ord. 6274 § 1, 2018)
