12.01.140  Enforcement.
A. The Director shall have the power and duty to interpret and enforce the provisions of this Code.
B. Notwithstanding any other provision of this Code, the Director has the authority to make reasonable accommodations for disabled persons in the application of this Code under Section 12.80.050.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022; Ord. No. 6508, § 1 (Exh. A), 11-4-2025)
