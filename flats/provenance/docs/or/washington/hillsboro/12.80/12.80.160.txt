12.80.160  Zoning and Comprehensive Plan Amendments.
A. Purpose. Sections 12.80.160 through 12.80.168 establish regulations, procedures, and standards for consideration of legislative and quasi-judicial applications to amend the Zoning Map and Comprehensive Plan map and text.
Such amendments may be necessary to reflect changing community conditions, needs and desires, to fulfill regional obligations, or to address changes in State law.
B. Applicability. These zoning and Comprehensive Plan amendment regulations apply to all applications for a Zone Change or Comprehensive Plan Amendments.
C. Application Types.
There are 4 types of applications under this section: Comprehensive Plan
Map Amendment; Comprehensive Plan Minor Text Amendment; Comprehensive Plan Major Amendment; and Zone Change.
As described in Section 12.80.166, there are 2 subtypes of Comprehensive Plan Major Amendment: (1) significant changes to the Plan; and (2) Plan revisions.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022; Ord. 6250 § 1, 2017)
