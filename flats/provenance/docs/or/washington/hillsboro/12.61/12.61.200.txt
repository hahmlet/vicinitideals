12.61.200  Applicability.
The standards of this Subchapter apply to properties within the Downtown Plan District boundaries as shown on Figure 12.61.200-A. There are 5 zones applicable within the Downtown Plan District:
A. SCC-DT Station Community Commercial - Downtown;
B. SCR-HD Station Community Residential - High Density;
C. SCR-MD Station Community Residential - Medium Density;
D. SCR-LD Station Community Residential - Low Density; and
E. SCR-DNC Station Community Residential - Downtown Neighborhood Conservation.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022)
