12.50.840  Articulation and Detailed Design.
A. Applicability.
1. The standards of this section apply to:
a. All non-residential and mixed-use development in light rail, mixed-use and urban center zones;
b. Commercial or institutional development in standard commercial or industrial zones.
2. In applicable development projects identified in paragraph 1, above, these standards apply to any portion of a façade meeting 1 or more of the following thresholds:
a. Facing, adjacent to, and visible from a street, major pedestrian route, or direct pedestrian way leading from a light rail station site;
b. Visible from and facing onto a park, plaza, or other public outdoor space.
B. Architectural Features.
Front building elevations shall avoid a flat appearance by including architectural features to provide variation and articulation.
Architectural features meeting this standard include, but are not limited to, the features listed below or similar features.
Window or entry cutouts on an otherwise flat façade do not meet this standard.
1. Cornices;
2. Bases;
3. Pilasters;
4. Belt courses;
5. Ornamental masonry;
6. Bays;
7. Recesses;
8. Arcades;
9. Display windows;
10. Recessed or detailed entries;
11. Fenestration; and/or
12. Similar architectural features requested by the applicant if approved by the Review Authority.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022; Ord. 6322 § 1, 2019; Ord. 6294 § 1, 2019; Ord. 6178 § 1, 2016)
