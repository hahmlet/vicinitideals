12.50.940  Crime Prevention through Environmental Design.
A. Providing an assessment from a CPTED certified professional that describes the site and building design elements that address crime prevention using the CPTED principles of territoriality, natural surveillance, access management, and maintenance.
(Ord. No. 6493, §§ 1, 2(Exh. A), 1-7-2025; Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022; Ord. No. 6508, § 1 (Exh. A), 11-4-2025)
