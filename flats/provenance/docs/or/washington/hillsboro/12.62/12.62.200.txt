12.62.200  Applicability.
The standards of this subchapter apply to properties within the Orenco Plan District boundaries as shown on Figure 12.62.200-A. There are 4 zones applicable within the Orenco Plan District:
A. Station Community Residential — Orenco Townsite Conservation (SCR-OTC);
B. Station Community Residential — Village (SCR-V);
C. Station Community Commercial — Station Commercial (SCC-SC); and
D. Station Community Industrial (SCI).
