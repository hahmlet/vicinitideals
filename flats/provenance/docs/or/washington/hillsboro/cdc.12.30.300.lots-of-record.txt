12.30.300  Exceptions to Non-Conforming Situation Regulations.
A. Non-conforming Lots of Record. Lots of record or the aggregate of contiguous lots of record
under the same ownership, which do not meet the dimensional or area requirements of the
applicable base zone, may be developed. Any new structure built on the lot(s) must conform to
the development standards for the applicable base zone (such as setbacks, lot coverage, etc.).
If the lot area is less than the minimum lot area for a single detached dwelling in the applicable
zone, any permitted residential use shall be limited to a single detached dwelling, a duplex, or
a townhouse.
