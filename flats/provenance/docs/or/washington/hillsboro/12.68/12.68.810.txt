12.68.810  Purpose and Applicability.
A. Purpose. These standards are intended to create attractive small-scale commercial developments that are compatible with residential neighborhoods.
B. Applicability. Except where noted in each subsection, the standards of this section apply to all commercial development in WHVS, as allowed under Section 12.68.110.A.
(Ord. 6440 § 1, 2023)
