12.68.350  Vehicle Parking and Loading: Location and Design.
The standards of Sections 12.50.350 and 12.50.360 apply within the WHVS Plan District, except as modified below.
A. Electric Car Charging Stations.
For new multiple-dwelling and mixed-use buildings with 5 or more residential units, at least 40% of all vehicle parking spaces shall provide electrical service capacity, as defined in ORS 455.417.
The electric car charging station spaces shall not be located in the right-of-way.
