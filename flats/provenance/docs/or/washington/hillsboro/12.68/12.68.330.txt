12.68.330  Exempt Parking.
The standards of Section 12.50.330 apply within the WHVS Plan District.
In addition, the following exemptions are applicable.
A. Exemption for Regulated Affordable Housing.
Regulated affordable housing units, as defined in Section 12.01.500, are exempt from minimum off-street parking requirements.
B. Exemption for Commercial Uses.
Commercial uses permitted per Section 12.68.110.A. are exempt from minimum off-street parking requirements.
(Ord. 6440 § 1, 2023)
