12.60.030  Hierarchy of Plan District Standards.
Development activities subject to this subchapter are also subject to the base zone standards in Subchapters 12.21 through 12.26, the special use standards in Subchapter 12.40, development and design standards in Subchapter 12.50, and any applicable overlay zone standards in Subchapter 12.27.
Where plan district standards conflict with other standards in this Code, refer to Section 12.01.430 for the hierarchy of standards.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022; Ord. 6322 § 1, 2019)
