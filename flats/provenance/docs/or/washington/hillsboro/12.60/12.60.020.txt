12.60.020  Scope.
Plan district standards are applied in conjunction with base zone standards (Subchapters 12.21 through 12.26) and Development and Design Standards (Subchapter 12.50).
Plan district provisions may modify any regulations of the applicable base zone or of the general standards unless the base zone or general standards specifically state otherwise.
However, base zone and general standards apply unless the plan district provides other regulations for the same specific topic.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022; Ord. 6322 § 1, 2019)
