12.60.010  Establishment.
A plan district is a geographic area for which special zoning regulations have been created by the City, either through adoption of a community plan in the Hillsboro Comprehensive Plan or by previously adopted provisions in Zoning Ordinance No. 1945.
If the plan district applies to the area included in a Community Plan, the plan district standards will implement the Community Plan.
Revision of plan district standards or adoption of a new plan district is a Type IV process pursuant to
Section 12.70.060.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022; Ord. 6322 § 1, 2019)
