12.64.310  Applicability.
A. The standards in this section apply to:
1. All new construction in the Amberglen Plan District; and
2. All redevelopment or expansions of existing structures that increase the Gross Floor Area
(GFA) of the existing structure by more than 20%.
B. Compliance with this section is not required for change of occupancy of an existing building: this provision supersedes the requirement of Subsection 12.50.410.B. regarding provision of bicycle parking with changes of use.
C. Unless otherwise provided in Section 12.64.340, all parking shall be located on-site on the same property as the use it serves, or in the adjacent public right-of-way.
(Ord. No. 6488, §§ 1, 2(Exh. A), 12-3-2024; Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022; Ord. 6322 § 1, 2019)
