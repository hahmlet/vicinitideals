12.64.940  Mixed Income Housing.
Twenty percent reductions in minimum residential density requirements in return for setting aside 10% of units for purchase price affordable to households at 80% of AMI or for rental affordable to households at 60% of AMI, as determined by the Review Authority.
Affordability at these levels must be assured for a period of at least 30 years through a development agreement with the applicant.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022)
