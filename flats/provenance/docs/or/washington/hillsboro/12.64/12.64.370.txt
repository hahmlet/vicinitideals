12.64.370  Bicycle Parking.
A. At least 2 bicycle parking spaces required by Table 12.64.320-1 shall be covered or in lockers consistent with Subsection 12.50.410.G.
Ten percent of all remaining required bicycle parking shall also be covered or in lockers.
B. All bicycle parking improvements shall comply with Subsection 12.50.410.F.
C. Residential units are exempt from bicycle parking requirements if each dwelling unit has an assigned, enclosed, individual garage or carport space at least 80 square feet in area.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022)
