12.27.610  Purpose.
The Special Industrial District (SID) is an overlay zone supplementing the provisions of the underlying I-P Industrial Park zone.
The purposes of the Special Industrial District are:
A. To protect and enhance development opportunities for industrial Uses which may require large sites in a planned campus industrial park setting;
B. To provide opportunities for small- and medium-sized industrial Uses, compatible with planned campus industrial parks, to locate near large single user industrial Uses; and
C. To preserve large lots for single major industrial Uses until such time as there is no demonstrated demand or need for such large lots.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022)
