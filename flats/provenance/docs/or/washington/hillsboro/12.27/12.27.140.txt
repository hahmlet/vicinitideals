12.27.140  Variances from RFO Standards.*
A. Definition. For floodplain management purposes, a Variance means a grant of relief by a community from the terms of a floodplain management regulation.
B. Criteria and Procedures.
A variance from one or more of the RFO standards in Sections 12.27.120, 12.27.125,12.27.130, and 12.27.135 may be approved by the Review Authority upon approval of a Variance.
The criteria for approval of a Variance from RFO standards are listed in Subsection 12.80.152.E.
An application for an RFO Variance shall be processed as a Type III procedure under the provisions of Section 12.70.050.
