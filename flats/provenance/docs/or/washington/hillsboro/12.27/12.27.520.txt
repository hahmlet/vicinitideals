12.27.520  Distinction from other Overlay Zones/Applicability.
A. Distinction from Other Overlay Zones.
The PUD overlay zone is unique, due to its creation as a result of an application from the property owner and a discretionary decision by the City, rather than as application as a response to existing site characteristics.
B. Applicability. The PUD overlay may be applied on 1 or more properties of any size in any zone, upon application by the property owner and approval by the Review Authority.
Development on the properties cited in Subsection 12.80.120.B. requires approval of a PUD overlay.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022)
