12.65.010  Purpose.
The South Hillsboro Plan District development and design standards reflect the City's goals and objectives for South Hillsboro and are intended to implement the vision, goals, policies and principles outlined in the South Hillsboro Community Plan to create a complete, connected and green community.
More specifically, the South Hillsboro Plan District provides standards for developing a residential mixed-use community organized around a town center and complemented by a village center.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022; Ord. 6110 § 8, 2015)
