12.65.620  Public Utilities General Requirements.
The standards of Section 12.50.620 shall apply within the South Hillsboro Plan District except that utilities are required to be undergrounded, with the exception of electrical transmission lines, either in the public right-of-way or in a public utility easement.
Public Utility Easement and vault locations shall be subject to review and approval by the City Engineer and the utility provider.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022; Ord. 6110 § 8, 2015)
