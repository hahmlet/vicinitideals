12.24.810  Purpose.
A. Create Opportunities for Neighborhood Commercial Uses.
The UC-NC zone implements the MU Mixed-Use Plan designation, providing opportunities to site a mixture of neighborhood-serving retail, residential, office, and research uses at moderate to high intensities to support primary uses within high-intensity areas.
B. Encourage Multi-Modal Transportation.
The UC-NC zone encourages non-automobile transportation modes, supporting and enhancing better use of the existing street grid pattern by pedestrians, bicyclists, and transit riders.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022; Ord. 6322 § 1, 2019)
