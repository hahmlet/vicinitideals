12.24.710  Purpose.
A. Create Opportunities for Mixed-Use Development.
The UC-AC zone implements the MU-UC Mixed-Use - Urban Commercial Plan designation, providing opportunities to establish a vibrant mix of retail, restaurants, civic, cultural, entertainment, and recreational uses complemented by secondary office and residential uses.
B. Create Active Street Frontages.
The standards of the UC-AC zone work to create focused activity served by transit and active street frontages along the High Capacity Transit routes that provide visual and physical connections to other activity centers.
(Ord. 6401 § 1, 2022; Ord. 6322 § 1, 2019)
