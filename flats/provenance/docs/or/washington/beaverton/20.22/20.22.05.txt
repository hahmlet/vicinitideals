20.22.05. Cooper Mountain Areas.

[ORD 4859; January 2025]
The Cooper Mountain zoning districts provide zones to implement the Cooper Mountain Community Plan with a range of housing types, commercial opportunities, natural resource protection, parks, trails, and transportation.
This section contains Residential, Commercial, and Multiple Use zoning districts.
Refer to Section 10.25 for the classifications.
[ORD 4859, 01/02/2025]
Effective on: 1/2/2025
