20.10.05. Corridor and Main Street Areas.

These areas of the City implement the Corridor and Main Street policies of the City's Comprehensive Plan and are identified on the City's Zoning Map.
Full urban services are to be provided.
Four commercial zones establish varied levels of commercial uses and residential densities.
