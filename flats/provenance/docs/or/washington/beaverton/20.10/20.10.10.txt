20.10.10. Purpose.

1.Neighborhood Service (NS)
The NS District is intended to provide minimal areas of service and convenience uses to meet the frequent needs of nearby residents.
2.Community Service (CS)
The CS District is intended to provide for a variety of business types compatible with and of similar scale to commercial activities found principally along the City's major streets.
3.Corridor Commercial (CC)
The CC District is intended to provide Corridors to develop into multiple use employment and service centers. 4.General Commercial (GC)
The GC District is intended to provide businesses requiring extensive land intensive outdoor storage and/or display of merchandise, equipment, or inventory.
