20.10.16. Floor Area Ratio in CS, CC, GC

Development shall have a maximum Floor Area Ratio as follows:
A. Within one-quarter mile north or south of lot lines abutting Allen Boulevard between Highway 217 and Murray Boulevard:
1. Maximum 6.0 if the site is near Frequent Transit Service and the site contains or the proposed development provides Regulated Affordable Housing.
A minimum of 30 percent of the residential units shall meet the definition of Regulated Affordable Housing; or
2. Maximum 4.0 if the site and development do not meet the thresholds in Section 20.10.16.A.1.
B. Not within one-quarter mile north or south of lot lines abutting Allen Boulevard between Highway 217 and Murray Boulevard:
1. Maximum 6.0 if the site is near Frequent Transit Service; or
2. Maximum 4.0 if the site is not near Frequent Transit Service.
C. For the purposes of this sub-section, “near Frequent Transit Service” means that any portion of the site is within one-half mile of the edge of right of way o streets with Frequent Transit Service or the center of tracks with Frequent Transit Service.
