20.30.05. Purpose and Applicability.

[ORD 4822; June 2022]
In the RMB and RMC zoning districts, all new development except for townhouses and cottage clusters is subject to the additional height limitations in Section 20.30.10.
The purpose of these additional height limitations is to help new development respond to the scale and form of existing neighborhoods.
[ORD 4822, 06/30/2022]
Effective on: 8/10/2026
