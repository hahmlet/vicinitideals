20.10.05. Corridor and Main Street Areas.

These areas of the City implement the Corridor and Main Street policies of the City's Comprehensive Plan and are
identified on the City's Zoning Map. Full urban services are to be provided.
Four commercial zones establish varied levels of commercial uses and residential densities.

20.10.10. Purpose.

1.Neighborhood Service (NS)
The NS District is intended to provide minimal areas of service and convenience uses to meet the frequent needs of
nearby residents.
2.Community Service (CS)
The CS District is intended to provide for a variety of business types compatible with and of similar scale to
commercial activities found principally along the City's major streets.
Beaverton Development Code
Date Printed: September 28, 2026 Chapter 20, ZD-11
3.Corridor Commercial (CC)
The CC District is intended to provide Corridors to develop into multiple use employment and service centers.
4.General Commercial (GC)
The GC District is intended to provide businesses requiring extensive land intensive outdoor storage and/or display
of merchandise, equipment, or inventory.

20.10.16. Floor Area Ratio in CS, CC, GC

Development shall have a maximum Floor Area Ratio as follows:
A. Within one-quarter mile north or south of lot lines abutting Allen Boulevard between Highway 217 and Murray
Boulevard:
1. Maximum 6.0 if the site is near Frequent Transit Service and the site contains or the proposed development
provides Regulated Affordable Housing. A minimum of 30 percent of the residential units shall meet the
definition of Regulated Affordable Housing; or
2. Maximum 4.0 if the site and development do not meet the thresholds in Section 20.10.16.A.1.
B. Not within one-quarter mile north or south of lot lines abutting Allen Boulevard between Highway 217 and Murray
Boulevard:
1. Maximum 6.0 if the site is near Frequent Transit Service; or
2. Maximum 4.0 if the site is not near Frequent Transit Service.
C. For the purposes of this sub-section, “near Frequent Transit Service” means that any portion of the site is within
one-half mile of the edge of right of way o streets with Frequent Transit Service or the center of tracks with
Frequent Transit Service.
[ORD 4879, 04/02/2026]
Effective on: 4/2/2026
