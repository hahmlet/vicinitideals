10.90. Saving Clause.

The repeal of any ordinance listed in Section 10.85. shall not affect or prevent application of remedies or punishment of a person for an act done or committed prior to November 17, 1978 and in violation of any ordinance repealed and listed in Section 10.85.
[ORD 4224, 09/19/2002]
Effective on: 9/19/2002
