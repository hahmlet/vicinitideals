10.60. Burden of Proof.

[ORD 4224; August 2002]
1. Except as otherwise provided, the applicant shall bear the burden of proof and persuasion that the proposal is in compliance with the applicable provisions of this Code.
2. Failure to comply with applicable procedural provisions of this Code shall invalidate an action only if it prejudices the substantial rights of the person alleging the error.
Persons alleging a procedural error shall have the burden of proof and persuasion as to whether the error occurred and whether the error has prejudiced the person's substantial rights.
[ORD 4224, 09/19/2002]
Effective on: 6/1/2012
