10.50. Authorization for Similar Uses.

The Director may authorize that a use, not specifically named in the allowed uses, be Permitted if the use is of the same general type and is similar to the allowed uses; provided, however, that the Director may not permit a use already allowed in any other zoning district of this Code.
Application for such a decision shall be processed as a Director's Interpretation, as provided by Section 40.25. of this Code.
