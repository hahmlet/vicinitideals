10.93. Hearings Officer.

The City Council may by resolution transfer any or all quasi-judicial powers and duties, including quasi-judicial administrative powers and duties, of the Planning Commission as herein provided to a Hearings Officer.
Prior to such action, the Commission, as appropriate, shall comment on and recommend to the Council which specific matters should be transferred to the Hearings Officer.
