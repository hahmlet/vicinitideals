20.20.16. Floor Area Ratio in RC-E, C-WS, TC-MU, TC-HDR, SC-MU and SC-
HDR

Development shall have a maximum Floor Area Ratio as follows:
1. Maximum 6.0 when any portion of the site is within one-half mile of Frequent Transit Service.
2. Maximum 4.0 when no portion of the site is within one-half mile of Frequent Transit Service.
3. Distance from Frequent Transit Service shall be measured from the edge of right of way of streets with Frequent Transit Service or the center of tracks with Frequent Transit Service.
