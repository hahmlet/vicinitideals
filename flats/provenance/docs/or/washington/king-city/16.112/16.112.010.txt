16.112.010 Purpose.
The purpose of the ROS zone is to accommodate park, open space and recreation facilities, including the existing golf course and appurte-nant headquarters, pro shop, and maintenance structures, which are operated by the King City
Civic Association. In addition, this zone may also be applied to protect and enhance natural resource areas, including but not limited to riparian areas, wetlands and the Tualatin River flood plain.
The zone implements the comprehensive plan policies and regulations that are intended to create, maintain, and promote these activities.
