16.112.030 Conditional uses.
A conditional use is a use, which is subject to a discretionary decision by the planning commission.
The approval criteria are set forth in Chapter
16.156. If a use is not listed as a conditional use, it may be held to be a similar unlisted use under the provisions of Chapter 16.82.
Conditional uses in the ROS district are:
A. Utilities (located within the UGB); and
B. Public safety facilities (located within the
UGB); and
C. Utility facilities necessary for public service (located outside the UGB).
Sanitary sewer facilities may be established or extended only as permitted by OAR 660-011-0060.
