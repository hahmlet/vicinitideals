16.94.020 Permitted uses.
A permitted use is a use, which is allowed outright, but is subject to all applicable provisions of this title.
If a use is not listed as a permitted use, it may be held to be a similar unlisted use under the provisions of Chapter 16.82.
Permitted uses in the R-12 zone are:
A. Residential home;
B. Manufactured/mobile home parks and subdivisions;
C. Family day care (family care);
D. Residential facility;
E. Parks and open space created as part of a subdivision or planned development; and
F . Dwelling, accessory .
