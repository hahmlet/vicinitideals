C. A public street right-of-way and improvement shall be required when more than six separate lots are served by a street or access drive.
D. Private residential access drives shall be provided and maintained in accordance with the applicable provisions of the uniform fire code.
E. Access drives in excess of one hundred fifty feet in length shall be provided with approved provisions for the turning around of fire appara-tus by one of the following:
1. A level, circular, paved surface having a minimum turn radius measured from center point to outside edge of forty-five feet.
2. A level, hammerhead-configured, paved surface with each leg of the hammerhead having a minimum depth of forty feet and a minimum width of twenty feet.
