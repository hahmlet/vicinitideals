16.82.030 Administration.
A. The city manager shall maintain a list by zoning district of unlisted uses approved by the planning commission and the list shall have the same effect as an amendment to the use provisions of the applicable zone.
B. Requests to approve an unlisted use shall be administered as a planning commission review in accordance with Article II of this title.
