16.82.040 Approval standards.
The planning commission shall approve an unlisted use application based on findings that all of the following criteria are satisfied:
A. The use is not specifically listed in another zone as either a permitted use or a conditional use;
B. The use is consistent with the comprehensive plan;
C. The use is consistent with the intent and purpose of the applicable zoning district;
D. The use is similar to and of the same general type as the uses listed in the zoning district;
E. The use has similar intensity, density and offsite impacts as the uses listed in the zoning district; and
F . The use has similar impacts on the community facilities as the listed uses.
(Ord. O-99-6 § 1
(part), 1999: Ord. 96-4 § 1 (part), 1996)
