16.104.020 Permitted uses.
A. A permitted use is a use which is allowed outright, but is subject to all applicable provisions of this title.
If a use is not listed as a permitted use, it may be held to be a similar unlisted used under the provisions of Chapter 16.82.
Permitted uses in the LC district are as follows:
1. Dwelling, multi-family;
2. Office;
3. Retail sales and service;
a. Sales-oriented,
b. Personal service-oriented, and
c. Repair-oriented;
4. Motel;
5. Community services;
6. Religious assembly;
7. Residential facility; and
8. Adult day care (family care).
