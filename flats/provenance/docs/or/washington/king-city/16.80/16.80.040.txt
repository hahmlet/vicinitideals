16.80.040 Zoning map.
The city zoning map is attached to the ordinance codified in this title and by this reference made a part hereof, bearing the signatures of the mayor and city recorder of the city, and entitled
"City of King City Zoning Map" dated with the effective date of the ordinance codified in this title.
It shall be maintained without change on file at all times in the office of the city recorder.
(Ord. O-99-6
§ 1 (part), 1999: Ord. 96-4 § 1 (part), 1996)
