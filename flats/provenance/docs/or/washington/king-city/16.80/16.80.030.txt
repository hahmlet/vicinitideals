16.80.030 Location of districts.
A. The boundaries for the districts listed in this chapter are indicated on the city zoning map which is adopted by reference.
The boundaries shall be modified in accordance with the provisions of this code.
B. The boundaries, legends, symbols, nota-tions and references of each of the zoning classification districts as depicted on the city zoning map are adopted by reference.
C. Unless otherwise specified, district and sub-district boundaries are section lines, subdivision lines, lot lines, center lines of street right-of-way, or such lines extended.
(Ord. 96-4 § 1 (part), 1996)
