16.80.010 Districts generally.
All lands, tracts and area within the corporate limits of the city area are included within one of the following described land use zoning districts.
The purpose of use, classification and uses of each tract within the corporate limits of the city shall be limited to those applicable to the zoning classification district within which such tract or lands are situated.
