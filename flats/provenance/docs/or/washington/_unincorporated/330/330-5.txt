330-5 Uses Which May be Permitted Through a Type III Procedure
The following uses may be permitted subject to the specific standards for the use set forth below and in applicable Special Use Sections of Section 430, as well as the general standards for the District, the Development Standards of
Article IV and all other applicable standards of the Code.
Approval may be further conditioned by the Review Authority pursuant to Section 207-5.
330-5.1 Airport - Section 430-7.
330-5.2 Ambulance Service - Section 430-9.2.
330-5.3 Campground - Section 430-25.
330-5.4 Cemetery - Section 430-27.
330-5.5 Change of Use from one Type III use to another Type III use.
330-5.6 College - Section 430-31.
330-5.7 Golf Course - Section 430-51.
330-5.8 Group Care Facilities (except day care facilities which are permitted as a Type II use pursuant to Section 330-4.7 J. and 330-4.8) - Section 430-53.1 through
430-53.3 and 430-53.5.
330-5.9 Heliport - Section 430-59.
330-5.10 Hospital - Section 430-65.
330-5.11 Park and Ride Facility - Section 430-89.
330-5.12 Membership Organization (not including public eating and drinking establishment) - Section 430-99.
330-5.13 Public Building - Section 430-103.
330-5.14 Public Utility - Section 430-105.
330-5.15 School - Section 430-121.
330-5.16 Transit Center - Section 430-137.
330-5.17 New Telecommunication Facilities with towers greater than 200 feet in height -
Section 430-109.
330-5.18 Religious Institution - Section 430-116.
330-5.19 Tree removal in areas identified in the applicable Community Plan as Significant Habitat, subject to Section 422-11.
