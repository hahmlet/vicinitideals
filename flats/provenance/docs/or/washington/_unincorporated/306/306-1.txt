306-1 Intent and Purpose
The intent and purpose of the R-24 District is to implement the policies of the
Comprehensive Plan for areas designated for residential development of no more than 24 units per acre and no less than 19 units per acre, except as otherwise specified for Middle Housing, or by Sections 300-2, 300-5 or 306-6.
(Ord. No. 885A, § 2.1.(Exh. 1, § 8.), 6-28-22, eff. 7-28-22; Ord. No. 890A, § 2.A.(Exh. 1, § 1.), 2-7-23, eff. 3-9-23)
