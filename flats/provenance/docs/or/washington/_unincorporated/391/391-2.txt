391-2 Applicability
391-2.1 The Bonny Slope West Subarea Overlay District provisions of Section 391 are only applicable to the Bonny Slope West Subarea of the Cedar Hills - Cedar Mill
Community Plan.
391-2.2 Unless otherwise specified in Section 391 or in the Cedar Hills - Cedar Mill Community Plan, all other provisions of this Code are applicable to the Bonny
Slope West Subarea Overlay District.
