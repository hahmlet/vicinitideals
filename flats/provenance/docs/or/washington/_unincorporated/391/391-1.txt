391-1 Intent and Purpose
391-1.1 The purpose of the Bonny Slope West Subarea Overlay District is to direct and encourage development that is primarily residential.
391-1.2 The intent of the Bonny Slope West Subarea Overlay District is to establish a development pattern that adheres to the provisions in the Bonny Slope West
Subarea of the Cedar Hills - Cedar Mill Community Plan.
Those provisions include the application of subarea-specific maps and the urban-rural compatibility requirements of Metro Ordinance No. 02-969B, Condition I. D.
