391-7 Land Use District Boundaries
If a land use district boundary divides a proposed lot into two or more districts, the entire lot shall be placed in the district that accounts for the greater area of the lot by the adjustment of the district boundary, provided that the boundary adjustment is for a distance of 20 feet or less.
The adjustment of the district boundary shall not result in any addition or reduction in density.
If an adjustment of more than 20 feet is required, the change in the district boundary may only be permitted through a Type II Planned Development, per the requirements of CDC
Section 404-4 (Planned Development).
