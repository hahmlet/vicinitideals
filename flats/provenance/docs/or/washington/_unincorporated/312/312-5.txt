312-5 Prohibited Uses
312-5.1 Structures or uses of land not specifically authorized by this District unless the structure or use has substantially similar use and impact characteristics to a use listed, as determined through the provisions of Section 202-2.2, or is identified under Section 201-2 (Exclusions from Permit Requirement).
312-5.2 Drive-In or drive-up establishments except as listed in 312-3.2 A.(2) and 312-3.6 312-5.3 New Dwelling units except as provided in 312-4.4 B.
312-5.4 Shopping Centers.
312-5.5 The location of service facilities such as high schools, hospitals, nursing homes, public assembly and high density residential development in airport approach zones.
Location of these facilities shall be avoided within any existing (June,
1983) airport year 2000 LDN 55 contour.
312-5.6 Auto wrecking yards.
