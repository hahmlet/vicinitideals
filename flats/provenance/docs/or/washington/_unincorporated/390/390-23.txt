390-23 Pre-Application Conference
A pre-application conference shall be required for all Type II and III applications, except for home occupations.
The purpose of the pre-application conference is to assist the applicant or representative by acquainting th em with the requirements of the Community Development Code, the North Bethany
Community Plan and other relevant criteria.
Pre-application conferences are not intended to be an exhaustive review of all potential issues.
The conference shall not bind or prohibit the county in any way from enforcing all applicable regulations.
