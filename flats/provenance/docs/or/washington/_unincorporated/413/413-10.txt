413-10 General Drop-off and Loading Space Requirements
413-10.1 It shall be unlawful to store or accumulate goods in a loading space if it renders it useless for loading and unloading operations.
413-10.2 Drop-off and loading spaces shall be located on the site and directly accessible to main structures.
The location of the drop-off and loading spaces shall comply with the requirements of Sections 403-2.3 E. (2).
