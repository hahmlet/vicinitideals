342-8 Access
All lots in this district shall either:
342-8.1 Abut a public street, or
342-8.2 Have an easement of record at least 30 feet wide at the street or as approved by the appropriate fire marshal.
342-8.3 Private driveways and private roads which are not subject to the standards of the Forest Practices Act shall comply with the standards of Section 428-3 and
428-4.
342-8.4 Roadways which are used only for forest uses and are subject to the standards of the Oregon Forest Practices Act, are not subject to the requirements of
Subsection 342-8. These roadways shall be subject to the requirements of
Subsection 342-8 and any other applicable Code standards if the roadways are used for uses other than uses governed by the Oregon Forest Practices Act, such as a residential dwelling.
