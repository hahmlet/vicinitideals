419-3 A fence, lattice work, screen or wall (includes retaining wall) not more than 7 feet in height may be located in any required side, front or rear yard, except as required by Section 418-3 (corner vision).
Where a rear, side or front yard abuts an arterial or limited access highway, fence height along the yard may be increased to 8 feet.
Any fence over 7 feet in height requires a building permit.
Any retaining wall over 4 feet in height requires a building permit (the height measurement of a retaining wall is from the bottom of footing to the top of the retaining wall).
