346-7 Access
All lots in this District shall either:
346-7.1 Abut a public street; or
346-7.2 Have an easement of record at least 30 feet wide at the street or as approved by the appropriate fire marshal.
346-7.3 Access roadways shall be approved, developed and maintained in accordance with the requirements of the appropriate fire protection ag ency for the geographical location.
Where no fire protection agency has jurisdiction, access roadways shall meet the requirement of the nearest or most likely fire protection jurisdiction to annex the property under consideration.
