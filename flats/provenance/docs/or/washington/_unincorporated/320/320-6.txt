320-6 Dimensional Requirements
320-6.1 Dimensional requirements for uses allowed through a Type II procedure:
A. Lot area:
The minimum lot area, except for a lot of record, shall be one acre.
B. Yard requirements:
The minimum yard requirements shall be:
(1) Forty foot front yard;
(2) Side and Rear Yards:
(a) Where abutting a residential district, the side and rear yard shall be no less than 20 feet;
(b) Street side and rear yards shall be no less than 20 feet;
(c) Except on corner lots, and as in (a) and (b) above, there are no required side or rear yards; and
(d) Additional setbacks may be required as specified in Sections
411 and 418.
C. Height:
(1) The maximum height for structures shall be 65 feet except as modified by other Sections of this Code.
(2) Normal building appurtenances and projections such as spires, belfries, cupolas, chimneys, ventilators, elevator housings or other structures placed on or extending above roof level may exceed the
65-foot building height limit to a maximum height of 80 feet.
(3) The height of telecommunication facilities are regulated by the
Permitted Use sections of this Land Use District, Sections 201,
430-1, 430-109 and other applicable provisions of this Code.
D. Lot Dimensions:
(1) The minimum average lot width shall be 100 feet;
(2) The minimum average lot depth shall be 150 feet; and
(3) The minimum lot width at the access point shall be 40 feet.
E. Maximum lot coverage:
The maximum lot coverage shall be 50% of the total lot area.
320-6.2 Dimensional requirements for uses allowed through a Type III procedure.
A. Lot area:
The minimum lot area, except for a lot of record, shall be two acres.
B. Yard requirements:
(1) Yard requirements shall be the same as those required for Type II uses (Section 320-6.1 B).
(2) Where a lot or lots abut more than one street, both street frontages shall be considered as front yards for yard, setback and landscaping requirements.
C. Height:
(1) The maximum height for structures shall be 65 feet, except as modified by other Sections of this Code.
(2) Normal building appurtenances and projections such as spires, belfries, cupolas, chimneys, ventilators, elevator housings or other structures placed on or extending above roof level may exceed the
65-foot building height limit to a maximum height of 80 feet.
(3) The height of Telecommunication Facilities is regulated b y the
Permitted Use sections of this land use district, Sections 201, 430-1,
430-109 and other applicable provisions of this Code.
D. Lot Dimensions:
(1) The minimum average lot width shall be 200 feet;
(2) The minimum average lot depth shall be 200 feet; and
(3) The minimum lot width at the access point shall be 40 feet.
E. Maximum lot coverage:
The maximum lot coverage shall be 60% of the total lot area.
(Ord. No. 826A, § 2.A.(Exh. 1, § 15), 10-24-17, eff. 11-24-17)
