356-5 Prohibited Uses
356-5.1 Structures or uses of land not specifically authorized by Section 356.
356-5.2 The use of a recreational vehicle as a residence.
356-5.3 The location of service facilities which house groups of people and public assembly facilities in airport approach zones.
Such facilities shall be avoided in existing June, 1983 airport year 2000 LDN 55 contour.
