356-1 Intent and Purpose
The intent of the MAE District is to provide lands for land extensive industrial uses in the rural areas of the county.
The purpose of the District is to provide land for farm and forest related uses needed to support the natural resource base and consistent with the rural character and level of services.
Land Extensive Industrial uses require large land areas, a low ratio of employees to land and sites free of natural hazards with immediate access to a collector, arterial or limited access highway.
For
MAE uses, lands must have an approved water and sewage disposal system and adequate capacity to accommodate drainage runoff of development.
