308-1 Intent and Purpose
The FD-20 District applies to the unincorporated urban lands added to the urban growth boundary by Metro through a Major or Legislative Amendment process after 1998.
The FD-20 District recognizes the desirability of encouraging and retaining limited interim uses until the urban comprehensive planning for future urban development of these areas is complete.
The provisions of this District are also intended to implement the requirements of Metro's Urban Growth
Management Functional Plan.
