340-6 UGB Proximity Standards
Applies to uses identified in this Code and in OAR 660-033.
Alterations or expansions of schools established on the subject tract prior to Jan. 1, 2009 are exempt from these standards.
340-6.1 No enclosed structure with a design capacity greater than 100 people, or group of enclosed structures with a total design capacity of greater than 100 people, shall be approved in connection with the use within 3 miles of an UGB, unless an exception is approved pursuant to ORS 197.732 and OAR 660-004, or unless the structure is described in a master plan adopted under the provisions of OAR
660-034.
340-6.2 Any enclosed structures or group of enclosed structures described in Section 106-205 or within a tract must be separated by at least one-half mile.
For purposes of this section, "tract" means a tract as defined by ORS 215.010(2) in existence as of June 17, 2010.
340-6.3 Existing facilities wholly within a farm use district may be maintained, enhanced or expanded on the same tract, subject to OAR 660-033.
(Ord. No. 877A, § 2.A.(Exh. 1, § 2.), 10-11-21, eff. 11-10-21)
