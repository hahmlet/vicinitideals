340-8 Creation of Lots or Parcels by a Standard Land Division
In addition to the standards listed below, all Standard Land Divisions shall comply with the applicable standards of Section 610 (Standard Land Divisions
Outside a UGB).
340-8.1 Creation of lots or parcels for farm use not less than 80 acres through a Type II procedure - Section 424-1.
340-8.2 Creation of a lot or parcel for a nonfarm dwelling through a Type III procedure -
Section 424-3.
340-8.3 Creation of a lot or parcel for a nonfarm use, not including a dwelling, through a Type II procedure - Section 424-4.
340-8.4 Creation of a parcel with an existing dwelling to be used for historic property through a Type II procedure - Section 424-5.
340-8.5 Creation of a parcel with an existing dwelling to be used as a residential home as defined in ORS 197.660, through a Type III procedure - Section 424-6.
(Ord. No. 855, § 2.A.(Exh. 1, § 17), 8-6-19, eff. 11-28-19; Ord. No. 890A, § 2.A.(Exh. 1, § 1.), 2-7-23, eff. 3-9-23)
