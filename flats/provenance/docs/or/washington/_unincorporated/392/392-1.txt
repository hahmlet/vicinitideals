392-1 Intent and Purpose
The intent of the Pedestrian-Oriented Mixed-Use Districts is to encourage development that is pedestrian-oriented and transit-supportive in areas that are generally within a half-mile of clusters of businesses and/or higher-density residential areas, and/or proximate to arterial or collector streets, and/or within Metro 2040 designated Town Centers or Transit Corridors.
The purpose of Pedestrian-Oriented Mixed-Use Districts is to promote vibrant community hubs through a complementary mix of residential, commercial and institutional land uses, along with development standards that encourage people to walk, ride a bicycle, or use transit for a significant percentage of their trips.
