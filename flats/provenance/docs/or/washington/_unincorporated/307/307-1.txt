307-1 Intent and Purpose
The intent of the R-25+ District is to implement the policies of the Comprehensive Plan for areas designated for residential development of 25 units or more per acre and no less than 20 units per acre, except as otherwise specified for Middle Housing, or by Sections 300-2, 300-5, or 307-6.
The purpose of the district is to provide areas for high density attached housing.
