344-8 Creation of Lots or Parcels by a Standard Land Division
In addition to the standards listed below, all Standard Land Divisions shall comply with the applicable standards of Section 610 (Standard Land Divisions
Outside a UGB).
(Ord. No. 890A, § 2.A.(Exh. 1, § 1.), 2-7-23, eff. 3-9-23)
344-8.1 Creation of lots or parcels for farm use not less than 80 acres or more through a Type II procedure - Section 424-1.
344-8.2 Creation of a lot or parcel for a nonfarm dwelling through a Type III procedure -
Section 424-3.
344-8.3 Creation of lots or parcels for nonfarm uses, not including a dwelling, through a Type II procedure - Section 424-4.
344-8.4 Creation of a parcel with an existing dwelling to be used for historic property through a Type II procedure - Section 424-5.
344-8.5 Creation of a parcel with an existing dwelling to be used as a residential home, as defined in ORS 197.660, through a Type III procedure - Section 424-6.
(Ord. No. 855, § 2.A.(Exh. 1, § 18), 8-6-19, eff. 11-28-19)
