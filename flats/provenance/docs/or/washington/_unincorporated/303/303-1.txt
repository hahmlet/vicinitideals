303-1 Intent and Purpose
The purpose of the R-6 District is to implement the policies of the Comprehensive Plan for areas designated for residential development at no more than six units per acre and no less than five units per acre, except as specified otherwise for Middle Housing, or by Sections 300-2, 300-5 or 303-6.
The intent of the R-6
District is to provide the opportunity for more flexibility in development than is allowed in the R-5 District.
(Ord. No. 885A, § 2.1.(Exh. 1, § 5.), 6-28-22, eff. 7-28-22; Ord. No. 890A, § 2.A.(Exh. 1, § 1.), 2-7-23, eff. 3-9-23)
