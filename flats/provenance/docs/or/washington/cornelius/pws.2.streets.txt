Driveway Locations and Spacing

              1.  Driveways on local streets shall be a minimum 100-feet back from the right-of-
                  way of an arterial, collector, or industrial street and 25-feet back from the right-of-
                  way of a local street.  This is shown in the drawing below.

              2.  Driveways on arterials, collectors, and industrial streets shall maintain a 100-foot
                  separation between driveways and from the right-of-way of arterials, collectors,
                  industrials streets, and local streets.

              3.  Driveway access to public streets requires City Engineer approval.  The approval
                  can be in the form of a Public Works Permit or Engineering Plan Approval.  The
                  City Engineer may require a traffic study if a proposed driveway may cause
                  safety issues.  The City Engineer may also impose conditions on the approval to
                  avoid potential traffic and pedestrian safety issues.
