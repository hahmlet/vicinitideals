§ 18.143.070. Intelligent transportation systems.

Intelligent transportation systems (ITS) manage and enhance operational performance through advanced technologies and management techniques to help relieve congestion, promote safety and provide suitable transportation strategies.

In order to provide for efficient installation of future intelligent transportation systems (ITS), all roadway improvement projects, including private development with frontage improvements, shall install three-inch conduit to support local interconnect infrastructure.
The location, design and type of conduit shall be approved by the city engineer.

(Ord. 874 Exh. (1)(B), 2006; Code 2000 § 11.40.67)
