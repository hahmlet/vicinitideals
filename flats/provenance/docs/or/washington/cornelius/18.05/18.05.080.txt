§ 18.05.080. Fees for land use applications.

A fee shall be charged for all review and approval procedures, land use permits and administrative actions governed by this title.
Fees shall be set by resolution adopted by the city council.

(A)

Fee Schedule Update. The city council shall update the fee schedule from time to time.

(B)

Incorporation by Reference. The fee schedule most recently adopted by the city council is incorporated by reference in this title.

(C)

Fees Due and Payable. Fees are due and payable at the time an application is submitted or at such other time as may be specified in this title.
The requirement to pay a fee is jurisdictional, and the city will not process an application without payment of the associated fee.
If an applicant pays a fee after submitting an application, the date they pay the fee will be considered the date they submitted the application.

(D)

The city may charge double the usual application fee for those who fail to apply for any permit or other approval required by the city.
The failure to submit a required fee with an application or a notice of appeal, including return of checks unpaid or other failure of consideration, may preclude the proceeding of that application or appeal.

(Code 2000 § 11.10.08; Ord. 2024-03 § 1 (Exh.
A), 2024)
