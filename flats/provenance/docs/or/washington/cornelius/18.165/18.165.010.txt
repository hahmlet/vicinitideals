§ 18.165.010. Purpose.

The purpose of this chapter is to protect solar access to solar features on lots designated or used for a single-family detached dwelling under some circumstances.
It authorizes owners of such lots to apply for a permit that, if granted, prohibits solar features from being shaded by certain future vegetation on and off the permittee's site.

(Ord. 810, 2000; Code 2000 § 11.40.37)
