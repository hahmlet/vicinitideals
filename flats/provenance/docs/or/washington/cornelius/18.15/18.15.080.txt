§ 18.15.080. Record of proceedings.

City staff shall be present at each hearing and shall cause the proceedings to be recorded electronically, by computer or in writing:

(A)

Testimony shall be transcribed if required for judicial review or if ordered by the hearing body.

(B)

The hearing body shall, where practicable, retain as part of the hearing record each item of physical or documentary evidence presented and shall have the items marked to show the identity of the person offering the same and whether presented on behalf of a proponent or opponent.
Exhibits received into evidence shall be retained in the hearing file until after the applicable appeal period has expired, at which time the exhibits may be released to the person identified thereon, or otherwise disposed of.

(C)

Included in the record shall be a brief statement that explains the criteria and standards considered relevant to the decision, states the facts relied upon in rendering the decision, and explains the justification for the decision based on the criteria, standards, and facts set forth.

(D)

A person shall have access to the record of the proceedings at reasonable times, places, and circumstances.
A person shall be entitled to make copies of the record at the person's own expense.

(Code 2000 § 11.10.38)
