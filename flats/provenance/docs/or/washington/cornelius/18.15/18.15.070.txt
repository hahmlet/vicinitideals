§ 18.15.070. Ex parte contacts.

The general public has a right to have hearing body members free from prehearing or ex parte contacts on matters heard by them.
It is recognized that a countervailing public right is free access to public officials on any matter.
Therefore, hearing body members shall reveal any significant prehearing or ex parte contacts with regard to any matter; the member shall so state and shall participate or abstain in accordance with CMC § 18.15.060.

(Code 2000 § 11.10.37)
