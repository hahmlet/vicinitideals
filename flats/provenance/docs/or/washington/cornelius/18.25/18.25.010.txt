§ 18.25.010. Purpose.

The purpose of the very low-density residential (R-10) zone is to implement the low-density residential land use designation and policies of the comprehensive plan.
To do this, the R-10 very low-density residential zone regulates the construction of detached single-unit dwellings and middle housing on existing lots, and provides design guidance for low-density residential subdivisions.

(Ord. 810, 2000; Code 2000 § 11.20.11; Ord. 2022-03 § 1 (Exh.
A), 2022)
