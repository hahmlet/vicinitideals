§ 18.195.230. W definitions.

"Wall" means a linear, exposed face of a structure, not projecting more than 12 inches.

"Wall sign" means a sign attached to or painted on the exterior wall of any building or other structure.
This includes signs attached to canopies, awnings, marquees or similar structures.

"Wholesale" means the bulk sale of goods generally for resale to a person other than the direct consumer.

(Code 2000 § 11.60.00; Ord. 841 Exh. 2, 2003)
