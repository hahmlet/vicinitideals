§ 18.155.010. Purpose.

It is the city's intent to encourage the use of both active and passive solar energy systems for heating air and water in homes and businesses, as long as natural topography, soil, or other subsurface conditions or other natural conditions peculiar to the site are preserved.
While the use of solar energy systems is optional, the right to solar access is protected.
Additionally, a goal of this section is to ensure that site plan elements do not excessively shade adjacent properties, creating a significant adverse impact upon adjacent property owners.
Thus, standards are set forth to evaluate the potential impact of shade caused by buildings, structures, and trees.

(Ord. 810, 2000; Code 2000 § 11.40.31; Ord. 2018-05 § 1 (Exh. A), 2018)
