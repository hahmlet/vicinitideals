§ 18.110.040. Housing Types.

A.

A housing type is not a use category.
It describes a type of development that can contain a Residential Use.

B.

A list of allowed, limited, and prohibited housing types in residential zones is provided in Table 18.110.3.
Terms and abbreviations used are defined as follows:

1.

Yes, allowed (Y).
Housing types that are allowed.

2.

Limited (L). Housing types that require a conditional use approval or are allowed subject to specific limitations.

3.

No, prohibited (N).
Housing types that are not allowed under any circumstance.

C.

All housing types are subject to the standards and provisions of the applicable development standards chapter or applicable plan district chapter, if any.
The applicable chapter for each housing type is provided in Table 18.110.3.

D.

All allowed housing types may be built on site or manufactured off site.
