§ 18.140.020. Applicability.

The Parks and Recreation (PR) zone is applicable to all city-owned lands intended as parks, open space, and recreational facilities and may be applied within all comprehensive plan designations.
City-owned parks, open space, and recreational facilities located in a plan district may retain or receive other than a PR zone designation if it better furthers the goals of the plan district.
In addition, other public agencies may request a PR zone designation for areas that meet the purpose of the zone.

(Ord. 17-22 §2; Ord. 18-28 §1)
