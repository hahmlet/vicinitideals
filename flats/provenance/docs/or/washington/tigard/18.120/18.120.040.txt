§ 18.120.040. Housing Types.

A.

A housing type is not a use category.
It describes a type of development that contains a Residential use.

B.

A list of allowed, limited, and prohibited housing types in commercial zones is provided in Table 18.120.2.
The terms and abbreviations in Table 18.120.2 are as follows:

1.

Yes, allowed (Y).
Housing types that are allowed.

2.

No, prohibited (N).
Housing types that are not allowed under any circumstance.

C.

All housing types are subject to the standards and provisions of the applicable development standards chapter or applicable plan district chapter, if any.
The applicable chapter for each housing type is provided in Table 18.120.2.

D.

All allowed housing types may be built on site or manufactured off site.
