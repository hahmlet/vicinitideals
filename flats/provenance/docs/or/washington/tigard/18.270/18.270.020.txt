§ 18.270.020. Applicability.

The standards of this chapter apply to quad development wherever this housing type is allowed as provided in the use and housing type tables in Chapter 18.110, Residential Zones and Chapter 18.120, Commercial Zones.
Additional standards apply in the River Terrace Plan District, as provided in Chapter 18.640, River Terrace Plan District.

(Ord. 18-23 §2; Ord. 19-09 §1; Ord. 22-06 §2)
