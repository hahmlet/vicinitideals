§ 18.720.020. Approval Process.

A.

A quasi-judicial annexation application is processed through a Type III-Modified procedure, as provided in Section 18.710.090.
Quasi-judicial annexations are decided by the City Council with a recommendation by Planning Commission.

B.

A legislative annexation application is processed through the Legislative procedure, as provided in Section 18.710.120.

(Ord. 17-22 §2; Ord. 18-23 §2; Ord. 19-09 §1; Ord. 20-01 §1; Ord. 22-06 §2)
