§ 18.720.010. Purpose.

The purpose of this chapter is to establish procedures and criteria for annexations, under the provisions of Metro Code Chapter 3.09 and Oregon Revised Statutes including, but not limited to, ORS Chapter 222.
The provisions of this chapter are intended to achieve the orderly and efficient annexation of lands to the city that will result in providing a complete range of urban services and consistency with the comprehensive plan.

(Ord. 17-22 §2)
