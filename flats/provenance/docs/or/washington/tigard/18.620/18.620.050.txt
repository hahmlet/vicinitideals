§ 18.620.050. Signs.

Signs. In addition to the requirements of Chapter 18.435, Signs, the following standards must be met:

A.

Residential-only developments must meet the sign requirements for the residential zones, Subsection 18.435.130.A; nonresidential development must meet the requirements of the MUC zone, Subsection 18.435.130.C.
Sign area increases are prohibited.

B.

The maximum height limit for all signs except wall signs is 10 feet.
Wall signs are not allowed to extend above the roof line of the wall on which the signs are located.
Height increases are not allowed.

C.

Freestanding signs are prohibited within the required S-4 screening.

(Ord. 17-22 §2; Ord. 18-23 §2; Ord. 18-28 §1; Ord. 22-06 §2; Ord. No. 24-05, 4/23/2024)
