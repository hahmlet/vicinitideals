§ 18.620.060. Access.

Except as provided below, all lots must have access to the public right-of-way in compliance with Chapter 18.920, Access, Egress, and Circulation.
Such access may be provided by lot frontage on a public street or by creating uninterrupted vehicle and pedestrian access between the subject lot and the public street.

(Ord. 17-22 §2; Ord. 18-23 §2)
