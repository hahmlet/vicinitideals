§ 18.620.010. Purpose.

The purpose of this chapter is to recognize and accommodate the changing commercial and residential marketplace by allowing commercial and residential mixed uses in the approximately 7-acre portion of the Bridgeport Village site that is within the City of Tigard as shown on Map 18.620.A.
Retail, office, business services, and personal services are emphasized, but residential uses are also allowed.

(Ord. 17-22 §2; Ord. 18-28 §1; Ord. No. 24-05, 4/23/2024)
