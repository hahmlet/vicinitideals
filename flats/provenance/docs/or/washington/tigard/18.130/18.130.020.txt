§ 18.130.020. Base Zone.

The IND zone prioritizes manufacturing uses, with limited motor vehicle servicing, off-site services, warehouse and distribution, and wholesale and equipment rental uses.
Some civic uses are also allowed.
Uses in this zone are intended to foster job density, accessible career pathways, and living wage jobs.
Industrial uses may have some off-site impacts including noise and odor.

(Ord. 17-22 §2; Ord. 18-23 §2; Ord. 18-28 §1; Ord. No. 24-05, 4/23/2024)
