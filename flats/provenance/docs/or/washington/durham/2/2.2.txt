2.2     Zoning Map.
The Zoning Map appended to this Code is a graphical depiction of the general location of the City’s zoning districts.

2.2.1   Zone Boundary.
The boundary between different zoning districts on the map is:

2.2.1.1          The centerline of the street when the district boundary aligns with a street;

2.2.1.2          The lot line when the district boundary aligns with an approximate boundary of an existing lot.
