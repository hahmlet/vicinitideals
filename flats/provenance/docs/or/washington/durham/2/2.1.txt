2.1     Introduction.
This chapter establishes residential, office, industrial and open space zoning districts that divide the City into areas where various land uses are allowed so as to implement the Comprehensive Land Use Plan.
Uses allowed in a zoning district have common functional or physical characteristics and are categorized according to the primary use.
The chapters that follow then describe the site and design regulations that apply to the categories of uses allowed in each zoning district and the standards and criteria by which applications for land uses are reviewed and decided.
