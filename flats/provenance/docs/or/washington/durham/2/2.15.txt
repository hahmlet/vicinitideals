2.15    Natural Resources Overlay (NRO) District.
The NRO district allocates land that may be included in a planned residential development within a single dwelling residential district and that should be preserved whenever feasible for protection and preservation of fish, wildlife and riparian habitat, natural water storage areas such as flood ways and flood plains and streamside vegetated corridors along waterways.

2.15.1  Uses permitted in the NRO district are those permitted in the Natural Resources district but only as part of a Planned Residential Development.
