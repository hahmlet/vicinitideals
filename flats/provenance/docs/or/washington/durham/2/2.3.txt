2.3     A use permitted outright in a zoning district is compatible with the other uses intended for that district and therefore requires only ministerial review for conformance to the site requirements of this Code prior to issuance of site work or building or similar other permits for that use.

2.3.1   Property may have more than one allowable use and may have such accessory uses as are customary and reasonable for the allowable uses in the respective zoning district.
