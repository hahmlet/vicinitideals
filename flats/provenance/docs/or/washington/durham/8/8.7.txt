8.7      Final Plat Approval.
The City may require such changes in a final subdivision or partition plat submitted for the City’s approval as are necessary for compliance with the terms of the City’s approval of the tentative plan for the same.

8.7.1    A final plat of a land division shall not be approved that does not include the survey- and graphical detail required by ORS 92.050 and as may be further required by the Washington County Surveyor.
8.7.1.1          Monuments shall be placed and a survey shall be submitted for recording in the form required by Oregon law.
All such monuments must remain intact before the City may grant final plat approval.
