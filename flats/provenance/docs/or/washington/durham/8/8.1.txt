8.1     Relation to State Law.
This Chapter provides the process and standards by which the City shall consider and approve the tentative plans and final plats of partitions, property line adjustments, replats and subdivisions, including land divisions to be developed as multi-unit or planned residential developments or business parks.

8.1.1   All terms used in this Chapter are as defined by ORS Chapter 92.
In case of any conflict between the requirements of this Chapter and those of ORS Chapter 92, the requirements of state law shall control.
